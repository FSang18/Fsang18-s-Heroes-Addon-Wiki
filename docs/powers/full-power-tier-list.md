# Full Power Tier List

The following list includes a full Tier list of all the Main powers currently in the addon. While we will do our best to keep this list up to date as new powers come out, there may occasionally be a power or two missing from the list. The criteria being used to evaluate the tier placement of a power are Combat-based Abilities, Traversal Abilities, and overall Versatility. **The placement on the tier list is solely based on FSang18's and the Tester's opinions on the above-mentioned subjects, and thus there may be areas that others do not agree with**. The powers are in alphabetical order within a tier's list, **not ranking within a tier**.
