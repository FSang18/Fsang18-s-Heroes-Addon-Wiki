# Combat Specialist Brute

![Combat Specialist Brute](../../uploads/04f9d6185978f61aba69d6cbb02df668/image.png){ width="70" height="70" loading=lazy }

Choose to specialize in a **Heavy-Based** combat system, focusing on your strong, but slow attacks, and enchancing your defense. You hit like a truck, but you move and swing like one too.

**Brute Skill Tree:**

* **Heavy Combat** - Passively increases your damage output in combat mode by 1. Cost: 2x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Heavy Combat Level 2** - Passively increases your damage output in combat mode by 2. Cost: 3x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Combat Brawler's Block** - This ability can be activated by pressing \[Ability 1\] keybind this allows you to block any damage for up to 5 seconds. Cost: 2x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Combat Brawler's Block Level 2** - Doubles the time you can hold your block. Cost: 3x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Powerful Leap** - This ability can be activated when sprinting, double jump in the direction you are looking at. On the landing you will be immune to fall damage, and you will create a non-damaging ground shake, but all entities within its radius will be knocked back. Cost: 3x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Powerful Leap Level 2** - increases Powerful Leap range and knocks back entities in the landing radius further. Cost: 5x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Brawler's Ironhide Defense** - This ability can activates if you held your Brawler's Block for at atleast 3 seconds, on activation, it will grant you Resistance II effect for 5 seconds. You cannot use Brawler's Block when you have Resistance effect. Cost: 3x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Ironhide Defense Level 2** - You will get Resistance III effect for 8 seconds after you held your block for at atleast 3 seconds. Cost: 5x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Double Fist** - This ability can be activated by executing Right-click Left-click Right-click combo this will make your next Left-click attack into joined-hand strike, which will knock away targets you're looking at. Cost: 5x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Follow Up Strike** - This ability can be activated by executing Double Fist combo and pressing spacebar after to leap into the direction you're looking at and dealing 8 damage to the entities in your way. Cost: 15x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Brawler's Grapple** - This ability can be activated by pressing \[Ability 2\] keybind, you can grab and carry any entity for up to 30 seconds in a 1-3 range. Cost: 5x Brute Brawler Skill Points
* **Brawler's Throw(Crouch)** - This ability can only activated when grabbing an entity, crouch and release your grab to throw your target at a great distance. Cost: 15x Brute Brawler Skill Points

**Related Information:**

While Brute Style possesses the most damage output, you will get slower attack animations and movement speed in combat mode as a tradeoff.
