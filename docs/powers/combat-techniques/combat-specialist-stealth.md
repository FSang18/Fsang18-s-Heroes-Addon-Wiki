# Combat Specialist Stealth

![Combat Specialist Stealth](../../uploads/1beefe3f77394557f6f0dd8ae07a908a/image.png){ width=65 height=65 loading=lazy }

Choose to specialize in a **Stealth-Based** combat system, taking control of the night to strike fear in your targets. Take down your targets in the shadows with secret.

**Stealth Skill Tree:**

* **Stealth Vanish (Crouch)** - This ability can only be activated in combat mode, you become invisible at light levels 0-8 while crouching, no entity will be able to see you even after you attack them. Cost: 2x Stealth SP
* **Stealth Vanish Level 2 (Crouch)** - You become invisible at light levels 0-14 while crouching. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* **Stealth Chokehold (Crouch)** - This ability can be activated by having both hands empty looking at your target and pressing Right-click, you will inflict Weakness V and Slowness debuffs, also dealing 1 damage every second to the target. While holding someone in a chokehold, you can move them around for a duration of 10 seconds. Cost: 2x Stealth SP
* **Stealth Chokehold Level 2 (Crouch)** - Doubles the time you can hold your target in a chokehold. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* **Stealth Damage (Crouch)** - This ability passively increases your damage output from attacks in your combat mode while being undetected. Cost: 3x Stealth SP
* **Stealth Movement (Crouch)** - This upgrade passively increases your movement speed while crouching, requires combat mode to be active. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* **Shadow Step (Crouch)** - This ability can only be activated by pressing spacebar button and looking at your target in a 5-block radius, and you will instantly appear behind its back. Cost: 3x Stealth SP
* **Shadow Stab (Crouch)** - After unlocking this ability executing Shadow Step will deal 5 damage to your target. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* **Stealth Target Tag (Non-Crouch)** - This ability can be activated by pressing your \[Ability 1\] keybind while looking at your target in a 30-block radius for 15 seconds. Tagged target gets glowing effect and will take additional damage from stealth attacks. This ability can be used every 10 seconds. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* S**tealth Target Tag Level 2 (Non-Crouch)** - Doubles the time your target is tagged. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* **Poisoning Feint (Crouch)** - This ability will activate passively and grant Poison II debuff to the closest target in a 1-block radius with a 15 second cooldown. Cost: 5x Stealth SP
* **Poisoning Feint Level 2 (Crouch)** - Doubles Poison debuff time. Cost: 5x Stealth SP

**Related Information**

* Warden cannot see you in **Stealth Vanish** ability. He will be able to sniff you. And **Shadow Step** ability can trigger sculk sensors.
* Stealth Style vanish works both at day and nighttime as it requires skylight value 0-8(14) meaning it can be used in other dimensions infinitely.
