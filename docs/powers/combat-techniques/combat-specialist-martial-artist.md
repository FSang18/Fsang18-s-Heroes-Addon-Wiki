# Combat Specialist Martial Artist

![Combat Specialist Martial Artist](../../uploads/6d3dd405745d6497884da79cca45fad7/image.png){ width=72 height=72 loading=lazy }

Choose to specialize in a **Martial Arts-Based** combat system, striking with quick, low damage based attacks. Fly like a butterfly, sting like a bee.

**Martial Artist Skill Tree:** 

* **Martial Artist Guard** - This ability can be activated by pressing \[Ability 1\] keybind, to guard yourself from incoming damage. Cost: 2x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Guard Counter** - This ability can be activated when you are using MA Guard, press Left-click within a second of Guard ending to deliver strong counter attack to the target which will also knock them back. Cost 5x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Jump Kick** - This ability can be activated by pressing spacebar button and either Left-click or Right-click to kick your target. Cost 2x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Jump Kick Level 2** - this ability passively gives your kicks knockback up to 5-6 blocks distance. Cost 5x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Double Jump** - This ability can be activated if you sprinting for atleast half a second and press spacebar, on your decent you need to press spacebar again will activate second jump to keep your momentum and leap you forward. Cost 3x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Wall Run** - This ability can be activated by sprinting, you need to touch wall blocks to run along the wall while slowly sliding down. You will not take fall damage if you have been wall running. Cost 3x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Uppercut (Crouch)** - This ability can be activated press either Left-click or Right-click to uppercut your target into the air. Cost 3x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Uppercut Level 2 (Crouch)** - This ability passively increases how high you uppercut your target, up to 6 blocks. Cost 5x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Break-Fall Roll (Crouch)** - This ability can be activated by crouching before you touch the ground, you will do a barrel roll to lower your fall damage. Cost 3x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Break-Fall Roll Level 2 (Crouch)** - This ability passively increases your fall resistance to allow you to fall and survive from greater heights. Cost 5x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Cartwheel Kick** - This ability be activated by executing Right-click Left-click Right-click Leftclick combo to flip over on one hand and kick your target, knocking them back at a low distance. Cost 3x Martial Arts Skill Points 
* **Martial Artist Cartwheel Kick Level 2** - This ability passively increases damage your Cartwheel Kick does at the end of your combo. Cost 3x Martial Arts Skill Points

**Related Information:**

Martial Arts Style gives you faster attack animations and the ability to jump up to 2 blocks in height passively while combat mode is active.
