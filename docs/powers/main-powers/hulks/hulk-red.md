# Red Hulk

![Red Hulk](../../../uploads/9e6c63bbe7b46d741c6f997f7d767194/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Hulk - Red"** is obtained via consuming a [Gamma Radiation](../../../items/gamma-radiation.md) and being near a explosion, this will grant you one of the Hulk variants at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 60
* Maximum Healing/Second: 2 HP / 5 seconds
* Armor Rating: 30
* Armor Toughness: 16
* Base Attack Damage: 24
* Sprint Speed: 200%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Not Transformed): Controllable Transformation"** - Use this ability to manually transform in and out of your Hulk form.
  * (Crouch use for reversing into human form when transformed)
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Transformed): "Strength-based Grab"** - With an empty main / offhand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "World Breaking Leap"** - Use your immense strength to leap in the direction you are looking. Hold down the activation key for up to 5 seconds to leap even further, depending on how long you held it. This ability will have a 3 second cooldown after use. Charging up to level 5 will create an explosion where you start your leap.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Thunder Clap"** - Clap your hands together to create a shockwave dealing Sound Damage to those nearby. If used on a fire, it can be extinguished.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Targeted Ground Smash"** - Leap up into the air, then create a non-destructive shockwave with your fist to knock targets in front of you back, and deal damage.
* S**lot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Controlled Overheat"** - Expel your Overheat energy into a controlled explosion, that is smaller than the uncontrolled variant, but does not deal damage to you. This will release 2500 energy when used

**Related Information:**

* This power **cannot** use any [Secondary Powers](../../secondary-powers/index.md) while transformed.
* This power **cannot** use any [Arc Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/index.md), use anything or hold any items in main/offhand while transformed.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has the following _passives_ while in Hulk form;
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
  * **Explosion Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* When transformed, this power can't wear armor and use both their mainhand and offhand
* This power can not be killed when in human form if Auto-Transformation is enabled but will instead change into Hulk form and gain a short burst of healing. Once the Controllable Transformation ability is unlocked, the automatic transformation cannot be toggled on/off. Once this happens you will be forced to remain transformed for a full minute.
* When below 50% HP you start producing energy for the overheat bar, the following energy marks spawn extra particles at the players location
  * 10k energy you start producing smoke and apply fire aspect on-hit
  * 15k you start producing black smoke,
  * 20k energy you start producing fire when walking
* **Max Overheat Energy: 25,000**
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Thaddeus E. Ross, a.k.a Red Hulk](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Thaddeus_Ross_\(Earth-616\)).
