# Controlled Hulk

![Controlled Hulk](../../../uploads/ffb3d617da68302ed13ddfeb31e0b9f2/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Hulk - Controlled"** is obtained via consuming a [Gamma Radiation](../../../items/gamma-radiation.md) and being near a explosion, this will grant you one of the Hulk variants at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 40
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 6 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 26
* Armor Toughness: 12
* Base Attack Damage: 23
* Sprint Speed: 250%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Not Transformed): Controllable Transformation"** - Use this ability to manually transform in and out of your Hulk form.
  * (Crouch use for reversing into human form when transformed)
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Transformed): "Strength-based Grab"** - With an empty main / offhand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Swift Thunder Clap"** - Fire off a Thunder Clap, quickly allowing for you to use it again after a shorter cooldown. This attack has a lower range, but will still grant sound poisoning to those hit. This ability has a 3 second cooldown.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Gamma Irradiated Fists"** - Infuse your fists with your Gamma Radiation for 30 seconds. Punching any target while enabled will deal wither damage over time to them.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Controlled Ground Smash"** - Quickly smash the ground at your feet for a smaller radius AOE attack that also knocks nearby entities back.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Controlled Gamma Breath"** - Channel your Gamma Rays as a potent beam which can apply wither to targets hit by it. When you release the beam, regardless of how long you've used it, there will be a 10 second cooldown before you can use it again.

**Related Information:**

* This power **cannot** use any [Secondary Powers](../../secondary-powers/index.md) while transformed.
* This power **cannot** use any [Arc Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/index.md) or equip any item in the curios slot when transformed.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has following _passives_ while in Hulk form;
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Explosion Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power can use both their mainhand and offhand at the same time.
* This power cannot be killed when in human form but will instead change into Hulk form and gain a short burst of healing. Once this happens you will be forced to remain transformed for a full minute.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Jennifer Walters, a.k.a She-Hulk](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Jennifer_Walters_\(Earth-616\)).
