# World Breaker Hulk

![World Breaker Hulk](../../../uploads/b5ec8faf479f90d6633382036a0d48ae/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Hulk - World Breaker"** is obtained via consuming a [Gamma Radiation](../../../items/gamma-radiation.md) and being near a explosion, this will grant you one of the Hulk variants at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 52
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP/ 5 seconds
* Armor Rating: 36
* Armor Toughness: 18
* Base Attack Damage: 30
* Sprint Speed: 230%

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Not Transformed): Controllable Transformation"** - Use this ability to manually transform in and out of your Hulk form.
  * (Crouch use for reversing into human form when transformed)
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Transformed): "Strength-based Grab"** - With an empty main / offhand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "World Breaking Leap"** - Use your immense strength to leap in the direction you are looking. Hold down the activation key for up to 5 seconds to leap even further, depending on how long you held it. This ability will have a 3 second cooldown after use. Charging up to level 5 will create an explosion where you start your leap.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Thunder Clap Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot.
  * **"Thunder Clap"** - Clap your hands together to create a shockwave dealing Sound Damage to those nearby. If used on a fire, it can be extinguished.
  * **"Destructive Thunder Clap"** - Wind up your clap more to demolish the area in front of you and apply sound poisoning. This ability has less range but hits harder than the base Thunder Clap
  * **"Full Powered Super Punch"** - Punch a target within range with a powerful, full force, strike. This ability scales with your strength stat.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): Ground Smash** - Create a non-destructive shockwave with your fists to knock targets in front of you back, and deal damage.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): Worldbreaker Mode** - Using your stored up energy, enter your Worldbreaker state, changing your moveset and increasing your Attack, Armor and Toughness stats. This will drain your energy when active, and will de-transform you for a full minute when deactivated whether done manually, or from running out of energy.
  * **Worldbreaker Crushing Grasp** - Grab an entity you are looking at in front of you. While held, the target will be squeezed, having damage dealt to them over time.
  * **Worldbreaker Destructive Leap** - Hold to charge a powerful leap in the air, up to 3. Every level of your leap higher than 1 will cause an explosion where you jumped from.
  * **Worldbreaker Massive Thunder** - Clap your hands together with all your might creating a large AOE force to knockback and damage enemies that are too close. This will also create an explosion centered on your location when used.
  * **Worldbreaker Radiated Aura** - Drain your Worldbreaker energy to create an aura of Gamma radiation around you. While active, you will push back any entity that gets too close to you, and deal damage to them.

**Related Information**

* This power **cannot** use any [Secondary Powers](../../secondary-powers/index.md) while transformed.
* This power **cannot** use any [Arc Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/index.md), use anything or hold any items in main / off hand while transformed.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* When below %50 HP you start producing energy for the Worldbreaker Energy.
  * **Max Worldbreaker Energy: 100,000**
* This power has the following _passives_ while in Hulk form;
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Explosion Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* When transformed, this power can't wear armor and use both their mainhand and offhand.
* This power can not be killed when in human form if Auto-Transformation is enabled but will instead change into Hulk form and gain a short burst of healing. Once the Controllable Transformation ability is unlocked, the automatic transformation can be toggled on/off. Once this happens you will be forced to remain transformed for a full minute.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Bruce Banner, a.k.a Hulk](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Bruce_Banner_\(Earth-616\)).
