# Siren

![Siren](../../../uploads/be73536079c2a7f218262d0af7ef6a57/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Atlantean Sea-Changed - Siren"** is obtained via right clicking a [Atlantean Artifact](../../../items/artifacts/atlantean-artifact.md). This will grant you one of the Atlantean powers at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: 3
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 170%
* Swim Speed: 375%

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Siren Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Siren's Call" -** Sing your Siren's song to pull all targets in a 10-block radius towards you. Any entity within range will be force to stay within 3 blocks of you, but while active you cannot move deactivate it or move until the song is over.
  * **"Siren's Shriek" -** Let out a horrendous scream to apply Sound Poisoning effect to targets hit by it.

**Related Information**:

* **This power cannot breathe above water unless in raining**.
  * Limit for staying above water is 60 seconds, after that you will begin taking damage until you get into water where you can breath.
* This power can walk in to bottom of the ocean.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Swim Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Nausea Immunity**
* This power has a faster block breaking while under water.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Hila, a.k.a Siren](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Hila_\(Prime_Earth\)).
