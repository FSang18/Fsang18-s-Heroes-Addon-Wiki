# Wright

![Wright](../../../uploads/e5eb1ddf1d6999705179f0e28b254868/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Atlantean Sea-Changed - Wright"** is obtained via right clicking a [Atlantean Artifact](../../../items/artifacts/atlantean-artifact.md). This will grant you one of the Atlantean powers at random chance.

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 16
* Armor Toughness: 3
* Base Attack Damage: 12
* Sprint Speed: 140%
* Swim Speed: 220%

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Block"** - Block incoming damage for a short time.
  * **"Punch" -** Hit your target with a powerful strike that will apply knockback and will scale to your attack damage.

**Related Information**:

* **This power cannot breathe above water unless in raining**.
  * Limit for staying above water is 30 seconds, after that you will begin taking damage until you get into water where you can breath.
* This power has a visible, physical change to their head, arms and legs.
* This power can walk in to bottom of the ocean.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Swim Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Nausea Immunity**
* This power has a faster block breaking while under water.
* This power is based of from a subrace of Atlanteans called [Wrights](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Wrights).
