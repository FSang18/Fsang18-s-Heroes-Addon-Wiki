# Royal

![Royal](../../../uploads/c13f49b07dde42ee8abfd40ec4e840b9/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Atlantean - Royal"** is obtained via right clicking a [Atlantean Artifact](../../../items/artifacts/atlantean-artifact.md). This will grant you one of the Atlantean powers at random chance.

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 9
* Armor Toughness: 2
* Base Attack Damage: 10
* Sprint Speed: 200%
* Swim Speed: 400%

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Strength-Based Grab" -** Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Marine Telepathy"** - Using this ability underwater allows you to summon a fish friend of yours. This sea creatures have different abilities and will aid you in and out of combat. Your fish friends are:
  * **"Sir Puffs-a-Lot" -** Summon a Pufferfish. When called, he will move towards the nearest, hostile target to attack
  * **"Inkognito" -** Summon a Squid. When called, they will emit a cloud of ink for 1 full second every 5 second.
  * **"Fin Diesel" -** Summon a Dolphin. When called, he will swim near you to help increase your swim speed. He can also be right-clicked to ridden.(You can't drive them)

**Related Information**:

* This power can breath underwater indefinitely.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Swim Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Nausea Immunity**
* This power have a faster block breaking while under water.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Arthur Curry, a.k.a Aquaman](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Aquaman_\(Arthur_Curry\)).
