# Ichthyoid

![Ichthyoid](../../../uploads/da58ed9fdc32c42782286c882fdd7ee8/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Atlantean Sea-Changed - Ichthyoid "** is obtained via right clicking a [Atlantean Artifact](../../../items/artifacts/atlantean-artifact.md). This will grant you one of the Atlantean powers at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: 1
* Base Attack Damage: 6
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Swim Speed: 525%

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Hydro Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Poison Claws" -** With empty main / offhands, toggle on / off your claws which increase your damage output, slightly. This will also allow you to dual wield and give the Poison Effect to any target you hit for 5 seconds.
  * **"Bioluminescence Blast""** - Use your ability to create a burst of light that will disorient and knockback targets hit by it. If used underwater, it can also apply Shock Effect on the target too.
  * **"Scale-Based Camouflage"** - Conceal yourself using your Atlantean physiology for up to 10 seconds at a time. This is not True Invisibility so your shadow does not disappear.

**Related Information**:

* **This power cannot breathe above water unless in raining**.
  * Limit for staying above water is 15 seconds, after that you will begin taking damage until you get into water where you can breath.
* This power has a visible, physical change to their eyes, arms, legs and back. You can adjust your fin and eye colors in the color slider.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Swim Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Nausea Immunity**
* This power can walk in to bottom of the ocean.
* This power has a faster block breaking while under water.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Dolphin](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Dolphin_\(Prime_Earth\)).
