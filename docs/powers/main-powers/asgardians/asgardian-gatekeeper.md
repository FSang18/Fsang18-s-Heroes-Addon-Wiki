# Asgardian (Gatekeeper)

![Asgardian (Gatekeeper)](../../../uploads/a33fdbdc7823f4b9a7b02ca568ea354c/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Asgardian - Gatekeeper"** is obtained via consuming an "[**Idunn Apple**](../../../items/idunn-apple.md)" which will grant an effect to transform you into a one of the Asgardian variant.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 36
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3.5 Second
* Armor Rating: 10
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 8
* Sprint Speed: 220%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Allseer Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"All-Sight"** - The Ability to toggle on / off your All-Sight, allowing you to see entities through walls from up to 100 blocks away. Scrolling while the ability is active can allow you to change between being able to see All, Players, Passive, Hostile entities.
  * **"Astral Projection" -** Project yourself out of your body for up to 10 blocks. Double jumping will allow you to fly / go through blocks below you. When the ability is released, or if a hostile entity gets within 3 blocks of your body, you will snap back to your body.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Summoning of Bifrost"** - While wielding **Hofund** in your mainhand, gain to ability to utilize the power of the bifrost to teleport yourself and anyone within a 5 block radius of you to a designated location.(This ability requires [Tertiary Hofund](../../tertiary-powers/hofund.md))

**Related Information:**

* **Gatekeeper's Sight -** With the help of your All-Sight, use the command: "Fsang.Coords" to find the coordinates and dimension of any player
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has passive **Night Vision**(below light level 10 and after unlocking the All Sight ability) and Y**ellow Eyes.**
* Max Age: **800 days**
* This power gains various stats based on the amount of Minecraft days passed without dying, and the abilities unlocked via the Combat and Defense skill trees (stats additions below are based on **Max Age**):
  * **Max Damage:** +20
  * **Max Armor:** +16
  * **Max Health:** +16
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Heimdall](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Heimdall_\(Earth-616\)).
