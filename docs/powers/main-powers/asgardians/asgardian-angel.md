# Asgardian (Angel)

![Asgardian (Angel)](../../../uploads/1aaf65363038aa516b38e758f602991a/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Asgardian - Angel"** is obtained via consuming an "[**Idunn Apple**](../../../items/idunn-apple.md)" which will grant an effect to transform you into a one of the Asgardian variant.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 32
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 10
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 10.5
* Sprint Speed: 275%
* Flight Speed: 1.38

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Tendrils" -** Fire off a tenril of Golden Ichor to pull your target towards you, applying Slowness II and Weakness II for 3 seconds.
  * **"Liquid Metal Beam" -** Fire a beam of pure Liquid Metal which can cause fires and burns targets hit.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Golden Ichor Weapons" -** Summon your Golden Ichor Weapons, divine weapons that will deteriorate and damage any that aren't of Asgardian decent. Select your weapons using the Ichor Weapon Wheel. Summoning a weopon will cost you 2 Saturation Points. This weapons have passive fire aspect. Summonable Ichor Weapons are:
  * **Xiphos, Sword of Stars**
  * **Golden Ichor Spear -** You can throw the spear with crouch+left click
  * **Golden Ichor Scimitar**
  * **Golden Ichor Axe**
  * **Golden Ichor Hammer**
  * **Golden Ichor Shiel**
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Asgardian Warcry Rally -** Unleash a powerful warcry that will grant all entities within 5 block radius the Strength II effect for 30 seconds. If the target(s) have any of the Asgardian powers, they will also be granted regeneration.

**Ascension: Quenn of Hel**

> When your Karma is -500 or less, you will passively transcend into your "Quenn of Hel" form, adjusting your stats and changing your abilities.

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Hel Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Hel Blast" -** Fire a blast of your Hel energy that deals 10 damage and gives Wither III for 5 seconds.
  * **"Hel Shadow Step" -** Teleport yourself up to 10 blocks in the direction you are looking with a five second cooldown.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Ichor Weopons" -** Summon your Ichor Weapons, divine weapons that will deteriorate and damage any that aren't of Asgardian decent. Select your weapons using the Ichor Weapon Wheel. Summonable Ichor Weapons are:
  * **Golden Ichor Spear**
  * **Golden Ichor Scimitar**
  * **Golden Ichor Axe**
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Hel's Embrace" -** Using your Hel power empower yourself in the battle, using this ability will give you Strength II and Regeneration effect for 15 seconds.

**Related Information**:

* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* **This power can breath in space using the Ad Astra Mod**
* Max Age: **150 days**
* This power gains various stats based on the amount of Minecraft days passed without dying, and the abilities unlocked via the Combat and Defense skill trees (stats additions below are based on **Max Age**):
  * **Max Damage**: +3.7
  * **Max Armor**: +3
  * **Max Health:** +3
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Aldrif Odinsdottir, a.k.a Angela](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Aldrif_Odinsdottir_\(Earth-616\)).
