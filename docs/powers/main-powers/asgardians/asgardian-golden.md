# Asgardian (Golden)

![Asgardian (Golden)](../../../uploads/1da7f142f4c7857e2473526272b4156e/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Asgardian - Golden"** is obtained via consuming an "[**Idunn Apple**](../../../items/idunn-apple.md)" which will grant an effect to transform you into a one of the Asgardian variant.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 36
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3.5 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 10
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 8
* Sprint Speed: 275%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Block"** - Block incoming damage for a short time.
  * **"Beam of Godly Light" -** Generate a beam of such intense, golden light that can set targets on fire that deals 8 damage.

**Related Information**:

* **This power can breath in space using the Ad Astra Mod**
* This power can understand any **language** spoken, no matter which dimension you are in after purchasing the **Power of the Allspeak** ability.
* The **Allspeak** ability allows for a permanent **"Hero of the Village"** effect.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow.Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* Max Age: **1,000 days**
* This power gains various stats based on the amount of Minecraft days passed without dying, and the abilities unlocked via the Combat and Defense skill trees (stats additions below are based on **Max Age**):
  * **Max Damage**: +25
  * **Max Armor**: +20
  * **Max Health:** +20
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Balder Odinson, a.k.a Balder the Brave](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Balder_Odinson_\(Earth-616\)).
