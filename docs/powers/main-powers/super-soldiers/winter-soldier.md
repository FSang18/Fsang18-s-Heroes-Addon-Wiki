# Winter Soldier

![Winter Soldier](../../../uploads/1098f0754edd183671a39654cca9c52b/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Super Soldier - Winter Soldier"** is obtained via [Super Soldier Serum](../../../items/super-soldier-serum.md) at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 4 seconds
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 8
* Sprint Speed: 140%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Leap"** - When on the ground, leap forward in the direction you are facing
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Enhanced Punch"** - Punch a target within range, knocking them back. Increasing your Strength skill tree increases the damage for the following values: 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 10
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Metal Armed Block"** - With any variant of an Arm Replacement equipped, you can block incoming damage. Using a Vibranium arm increased the amount of time you can block from 5 seconds to 10 seconds.

**Related Information**:

* This power gains water breathing for 30 seconds when under water.
* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Gamma Radiation Effect Immunity**
  * **Nausea Effect Immunity**
  * **Strength Effect Immunity**
  * **Speed Effect Immunity**
  * **Regeneration Effect Immunity**
* This power can gain a passive +2 attack when using an arm replacement, and a +4 armor rating when the arm is a Vibranium arm.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [James Buchanan Barnes, a.k.a Winter Soldier.](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/James_Buchanan_Barnes_\(Earth-616\))
