# Goblin

![Goblin](../../../uploads/f990ced65376177f540d778273171434/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

**"Super Soldier - Goblin"** is obtained via [Super Soldier Serum](../../../items/super-soldier-serum.md) at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 32
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 8
* Sprint Speed: 155%
* Flight Speed: N / A

Main Abilities:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Leap"** - When on the ground, leap forward in the direction you are facing
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Grab"** - Grab a target in front of you to hold them in place.

**Related Information:**

* Due to your Goblin Metabolism, if you have poison, wither, weakness or nausea you will begin healing 1 heart per second, gain +4 damage and +6 armor.
* This power gains water breathing for 30 seconds when under water.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Norman Osborn, a.k.a Green Goblin](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Norman_Osborn_\(Earth-616\)).
