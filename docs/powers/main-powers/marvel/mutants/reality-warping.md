The "Reality Warping" power is obtained via X-gene item at random chance

Stats:
	
* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 0.7 /s
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 1.6 b/s
* Flight Speed 6.2 b/s

Slot 1 (Crouch): "Psionic Abilities" - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
* "Psionic Strike" - Directly attack a target's mind within 6 blocks. This will deal -4 Psionic damage to the target when hit.
* "Psionic Read" - Use your telepathic reach to reveal the information of Entities around you in a 30 block radius. If you are using Cerebro to enhance your abilities, you will also be able to read any powers targets in range have.
* "Psionic Sight" - Use your telepathic reach to see through another target's eyes. You will be able to hop up to 3 times into different targets.
* "Telekinetic Hold" - Telekinetically grab any entity or block within 8 blocks of you. Scrolling while this is enabled will allow you to bring the target closer or further.
* "Telekinetic Repulsion" - Telekinetically repel all targets within 3 blocks of you away from you.
* "Telekinetic Barrage" - Lift, then launch up to 5 blocks within a 6 block radius of you at your target for 4 damage per hit.

Slot 2 (Crouch): "Energy Manipulation" - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
* "Reality Conversion (Multiversal)" - Dip into your ability to rewrite reality to convert any item in your mainhand into its multiversal counterpart, if it has any.
*  "Reality Conversion (Store)" - Use some of your energy to register items within your mental database. This will consume the item being registered, and will not retain any enchantments / nbt data. Items entered in the Argon mod's config file blacklist cannot be saved for creation.
*  "Reality Conversion (Create)" - Use some of your energy to  create items within your mental database. Once registered, each item has a max of 5 times it can be created before needing to be re-registered in your mental database.
*  "Energy Beam" - Manipulate your energy to fire off a beam of raw reality energy.
*  "Energy Shield" - Manipulate your energy to create a shield of raw reality energy in front of you. With this shield, you will be able to block up to 50 damage in front of you before it breaks.
*  "Energy Explosion" - Manipulate the energy inside you by charging it up for 5 seconds, then exploding it out of you harming everything within a 5 block radius of you.

Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Dimensional Manipulation" - Open the ability wheel to use your abilities in your third slot. You need to hover over the abilities and click them for using it. The Abilities for the third slots are:
* "Spatial Travel (Short)" - Fold the distance between you and a location up to 15 blocks in the direction you are looking to appear there instantly.
* "Pocket Dimension: Storage" - Tap into the power of your Pocket Dimension to store up to 32 items in a controlled space that only you have access to.
* "Pocket Dimension: Travel" - Create a small portal to send yourself to your Pocket Dimension. Using this ability while in the Pocket Dimension will return you to where you were when you left.
Gain the ability to open a menu to create portals that allow you and others to travel throughout different dimensions.

Slot 4 (Crouch): "Reality Manipulation" - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fourth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fourth slots are:
* "Reconstruction" - Using the item(s) in your mainhand, convert it into anything that uses it as a crafting material. You will need the amount of said item used in that attempted item's crafting recipe to reconstruct it.
* "State Changer" - Alter the reality of a targeted block to change the blockstate of any block you are looking at between any of its available states.
* "Compact" - Select an area to alter into a compact item. Placing the item on a block will return it to it's full size.
* "Biome Shift" - Transform reality around you in a 12 block radius to that of a random biome. Those who are caught in the dome with you will not be able to escape / break any blocks until the ability is released. Based on the biome you changed to, have the following effects granted to all within the altered reality:
	* Plains: Regeneration
	* Desert: Hunger
	* Snowy Taiga: Freeze
	* Dripstone Caves: Fire resistance
	* Deep Dark: Blindness
	* Nether Wastes: Burning
  * Soul Sand Valley: Wither
  * Basalt Deltas: Weakness
  * End Barrens: Saturation

Slot 5 (Crouch): "Cosmic Manipulaiton" - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fifth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fifth slots are:
* "Cosmic Conduit" - Enter a meditative state to passively pull in Cosmic energy from around you to rapidly regenerate your reality energy bar, and create a light source around you. You will not be able to move, and your flight speed will be reduced by 90% while this ability is enabled, and you will also take 2x damage from all sources.
* "Cosmic Herald" - At the cost of a significant amount of your stored energy, grant the target you are looking at the Cosmic Awakening effect which can grant them access to the abilities of your Cosmic Herald.
* "Cosmic Resonance" - Drain your Reality Energy to grant the Regeneration, Resistance and Strength effects on all Heralds within a 20 block radius. This will also grant the Wither Effect to all non-heralds within a 10 block radius.
* "Cosmic Energy Surge" - Drain your Reality Energy to slow all targets within a 5 block radius around you, using your Cosmic Energy to deal damage to them over time.

Relevant information
* This power has 1000 energy
* This power is loosely based on Franklin Richards, a.k.a Powerhouse
* Cerebro gives buffs to certain moves when worn
* There are 4 abilities that add boosts to you when in the Pocket Dimension
	* Pocket Dimension: Healing adds an additional 1 heart/ 2 secs
	* Pocket Dimension: Health adds an additional 6 hp
	* Pocket Dimension: Energy which adds an additional +30 energy/ sec
	* Pocket Dimension: Saturation which adds an additional +1 saturation/ 3 secs
* This powers one of the two powers that can travel other dimensions.
