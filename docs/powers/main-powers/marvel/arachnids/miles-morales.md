Arachnid - Miles Morales

* Maximum Health: 38
* Maximum Healing / Second: 0.3/s
* Armor Rating: 7
* Armor Toughness: 3
* Base Attack Damage: 9
* Sprint Speed: 4.3 b/s
* Flight Speed: N/A

			Arachnid Abilities

Spider Sense: Detect any danger within 20 blocks of you. Additionally, player that deal damage to you will trigger your spider sense for up to 10 seconds after they hit you. The direction the danger is coming from will be represented by the 
angle of the color displayed on your screen.

Arachnid Mobility: Crouch when sprinting to slide, knocking back entities in your path. You can also do a double jump by pressing the jump key while in the air.

Arachnid Adhesion: By enhance the inter-atomic attractive forces between your skin and a surface, sharply increase friction to stick to/ climb on any surface. Wall Crawling can be toggled on/off with key bind set.

Arachnid Carry: Using your enhanced strength, and with empty main/off hands, carry the target in front of you.

Arachnid Dodge: Using your enhanced mobility, dodge in the direction you are moving to avoid incoming damage.

Arachnid Guard: Guard up to 10 damage from the direction you are looking. Having your guard broken will lock it out for 3 seconds.
			
			Invis Abilities

Fight or Flight: Unlock the ability to turn almost completely invisible to those around you whenever your Spider-Sense is going off, so long as there are no other living entities within 3 blocks of you.

Camouflage: Unlock the ability to fully become invisible to those around you at will. You will be able to stay invisible for 15 seconds with a cooldown of 3 seconds after releasing it,

Vanishing Dodge: After using the Dodge ability, become invisible for 3 seconds.
			
			Venom Abilities

Venom Punch: Concentrate your Bioelectricity energy into one hand and shock any target you hit with your bare hand.

Venom Leap: Using an explosion of your Venom energy, leap in the direction you are looking to shock all entities within a 3 block radius of you for 5 seconds when you do.

Venom Smash: Smash your Venom-enabled hands into the ground, knocking all entities around you back and shocking them for 8 seconds.

			Enhanced Bioelectricity

Chain Lightning: With your enhanced bioelectricity, launch a blast of blue bioelectricity at a target within 5 blocks of you to have it then jump to the nearest target within 10 blocks up to 3 times total, shocking each target it jumps to.

Reverse Flux: Fire off your blue Bioelectricity to all entities within a 4 block radius to pull them towards you.

Bioelectric Blade: Focus your bioelectricity to make a blade of lightning in your hand. While enabled, you will have sweeping edge with this blade. This will add +4 to your damage output, and apply shock to those hit.

Galvanized: Fire an Venom-Charged attack of blue bioelectricity that launches, then holds the target in place. Any other entity that gets within 2 blocks of the galvanized target will become shocked.

Relevant info:
* This variant has a bioelectricity energy bar which will passively refill over time.
* This power is based off of Miles Gonzalo Morales, a.k.a Spider-Man.
