### Arachnid Abilities { #arachnid-abilities }

<p class="slot-intro">Spider-like senses and mobility for getting around, climbing and avoiding danger.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/spider-sense.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Spider Sense

</div>

Detect any danger within 20 blocks of you. Players that deal damage to you also trigger your spider sense for up to 10 seconds after they hit you. The direction the danger is coming from is shown by the angle of the color displayed on your screen.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Range <b>20 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Trigger on hit <b>10 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/arachnid-dodge.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Dodge

</div>

Using your enhanced mobility, dodge in the direction you are moving to avoid incoming damage.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Direction <b>Where you move</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/arachnid-mobility.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Mobility

</div>

Crouch while sprinting to slide, knocking back entities in your path. You can also double jump by pressing the jump key while in the air.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Slide <b>Crouch while sprinting</b></span><span class="chip">Double jump <b>Jump in air</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/arachnid-adhesion.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Adhesion

</div>

By enhancing the inter-atomic attractive forces between your skin and a surface, you sharply increase friction to stick to and climb any surface. Wall Crawling can be toggled on and off with a key bind.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Toggle <b>Key bind</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/arachnid-miles-morales/arachnid-carry.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Carry

</div>

Using your enhanced strength, and with empty main and off hands, carry the target in front of you.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Requires <b>Empty hands</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

### Stealth { #stealth }

<p class="slot-intro">Turn invisible to those around you.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/camouflage.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Camouflage

</div>

Fully become invisible to those around you at will. You can stay invisible for 15 seconds, with a cooldown of 3 seconds after releasing it.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Duration <b>15 seconds</b></span><span class="chip">Cooldown <b>3 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/vanishing-dodge.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Vanishing Dodge

</div>

After using the Dodge ability, become invisible for 3 seconds.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Trigger <b>After Dodge</b></span><span class="chip">Duration <b>3 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/arachnid-miles-morales/fight-or-flight.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Fight or Flight

</div>

Turn almost completely invisible to those around you whenever your Spider Sense is going off, so long as there are no other living entities within 3 blocks of you.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Trigger <b>Spider Sense active</b></span><span class="chip">Requires <b>No entities within 3 blocks</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

### Venom { #venom }

<p class="slot-intro">Guard against incoming damage and strike back with Venom energy.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/arachnid-guard.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Guard

</div>

Guard up to 10 damage from the direction you are looking. Having your guard broken locks it out for 3 seconds.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Guards <b>Up to 10 damage</b></span><span class="chip">Lockout <b>3 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/venom-punch.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Venom Punch

</div>

Concentrate your bioelectric energy into one hand and shock any target you hit with your bare hand.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Effect <b>Shock</b></span><span class="chip">Requires <b>Bare hand</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/venom-leap.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Venom Leap

</div>

Using an explosion of Venom energy, leap in the direction you are looking, shocking all entities within a 3 block radius of you for 5 seconds when you do.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Radius <b>3 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Shock <b>5 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/arachnid-miles-morales/venom-smash.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Venom Smash

</div>

Smash your Venom-enabled hands into the ground, knocking all entities around you back and shocking them for 8 seconds.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Effect <b>Knockback</b></span><span class="chip">Shock <b>8 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div class="power-section cat-bioelectricity" markdown>

### Bioelectricity { #bioelectricity }

<p class="slot-intro">Enhanced bioelectricity, powered by an energy bar that refills passively over time.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/chain-lightning.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Chain Lightning

</div>

Launch a blast of blue bioelectricity at a target within 5 blocks of you. It then jumps to the nearest target within 10 blocks, up to 3 times total, shocking each target it hits.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Range <b>5 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Jump range <b>10 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Jumps <b>3 total</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/reverse-flux.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Reverse Flux

</div>

Fire off your blue bioelectricity to all entities within a 4 block radius to pull them towards you.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Radius <b>4 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Effect <b>Pull</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-miles-morales/bioelectric-blade.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Bioelectric Blade

</div>

Focus your bioelectricity to form a blade of lightning in your hand. While enabled, you have the sweeping edge effect with this blade, +4 damage output, and shock applied to those hit.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Damage <b>+4</b></span><span class="chip">Effect <b>Sweeping edge · Shock</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power/pages/arachnid-miles-morales/galvanized.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Galvanized

</div>

Fire a Venom-charged attack of blue bioelectricity that launches, then holds the target in place. Any other entity that gets within 2 blocks of the galvanized target becomes shocked.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Effect <b>Holds target in place</b></span><span class="chip">Shock radius <b>2 blocks</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

</div>
