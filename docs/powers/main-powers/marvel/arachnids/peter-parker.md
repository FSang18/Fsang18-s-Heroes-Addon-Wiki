### Spider-Totem { #spider-totem }

<p class="slot-intro">Being one of the Spider-Totems connects you to the Web of Life and Destiny.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/spider-sense.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Spider Sense

</div>

Detect any danger within 20 blocks of you. Players that deal damage to you also trigger your spider sense for up to 10 seconds after they hit you. The direction the danger is coming from is shown by the angle of the color displayed on your screen.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Range <b>20 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Trigger on hit <b>10 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/web-of-life.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Web of Life

</div>

Passively sense any spider, or player with an Arachnid-type power, within 20 blocks of you, even through walls. You can also walk through Cobwebs without any issue.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Range <b>20 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Passive <b>Always on</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/great-weavers-favor.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Great Weaver's Favor

</div>

Channel the powers of the Great Weaver to grant all arachnids within 10 blocks (including yourself) Regeneration II.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Range <b>10 blocks</b></span><span class="chip">Effect <b>Regeneration II</b></span><span class="chip">Duration <b>5 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

### Arachnid Movement { #arachnid-movement }

<p class="slot-intro">Enhanced mobility for getting around quickly and climbing anything.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-mobility.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Mobility

</div>

Crouch while sprinting to slide, knocking back entities in your path. You can also double jump by pressing the jump key while in the air.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Slide <b>Crouch while sprinting</b></span><span class="chip">Double jump <b>Jump in air</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-adhesion.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Adhesion

</div>

By enhancing the inter-atomic attractive forces between your skin and a surface, you sharply increase friction to stick to and climb any surface. Wall Crawling can be toggled on and off with a key bind.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Toggle <b>Key bind</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-dodge.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Dodge

</div>

Using your enhanced mobility, dodge in the direction you are moving to avoid incoming damage.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Direction <b>Where you move</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

<div class="power-section webbing" markdown>

### Organic Webbing { #organic-webbing }

<p class="slot-intro">Webbing produced by your own body. It draws on your Organic Webbing and costs no Web Fluid. This is one of the few Arachnid powers with organic webs, the other being Silk.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/organic-web-zip.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Organic Web Zip

</div>

Fire a Web-Line at any block to pull yourself towards it. Firing at another entity pulls that entity towards you instead.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Block <b>Pulls you</b></span><span class="chip">Entity <b>Pulls it to you</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/organic-web-swinging.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Organic Web Swinging

</div>

Create Organic Webbing to swing from. It is turned off by default, and you can switch to the assisted or freeform swing modes with the "Swing Mode Change" ability.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Default <b>Off</b></span><span class="chip">Modes <b>Assisted · Freeform</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/organic-web-shot.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Organic Web Shot

</div>

Fire small shots of Organic Webbing, alternating from each arm, to deal low damage and give your target the Webbed effect. Unlike the Web-Shooters, this costs no Web Fluid.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Damage <b>Low</b></span><span class="chip">Effect <b>Webbed III</b></span><span class="chip">Duration <b>5 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/organic-web-impact.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Organic Web Impact

</div>

Having refined your Organic Webbing further, fire a larger shot to deal more damage, knock your target back, and give them the Webbed effect. Costs no Web Fluid.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Damage <b>Higher</b></span><span class="chip">Knockback <b>Yes</b></span><span class="chip">Effect <b>Webbed VI</b></span><span class="chip">Duration <b>10 seconds</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability wide" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/web-blossom.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Web Blossom

</div>

Having pushed your Arachnid Mutation to its next stage, launch yourself into the air and spin rapidly, unleashing a burst of Organic Webbing in every direction to overwhelm and restrain those around you. Costs no Web Fluid.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Launch <b>Into the air</b></span><span class="chip">Burst <b>Every direction</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>

<div class="power-section mutation" markdown>

### Arachnid Mutation { #arachnid-mutation }

<p class="slot-intro">Extended time with mutated D.N.A. unlocks newly acquired capabilities.</p>

<div class="ability-grid" markdown>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-communication.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Communication

</div>

Because you are one of the Spider-Totems, connected to the Web of Life and Destiny, all Spiders and Cave Spiders will ignore you.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Affects <b>Spiders · Cave Spiders</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-mutation-organic-emergence.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Mutation – Organic Emergence

</div>

Your powers are facing an emergence of newly acquired capabilities. When your health is below 50%, you gain increased attack damage but also begin to automatically attack anything within 5 blocks of you.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Health <b>Below 50%</b></span><span class="chip">Attack damage <b>+2</b></span><span class="chip">Auto-attack <b>5 blocks</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-mutation-arachnid-evolution.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Mutation – Arachnid Evolution

</div>

Your arachnid-based abilities develop further. In addition to Organic Emergence, your spider sense becomes much more accurate at telling where entities around you are, at the cost of your senses being overloaded when it is activated in your mutated state, until mastered.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Spider Sense <b>More accurate</b></span><span class="chip">Drawback <b>Sensory overload until mastered</b></span></div>

</div>

<div class="ability" markdown>

<div class="ab-head" markdown>

![](../../../../uploads/power-images/arachnid-peter-parker/arachnid-mutation-mutation-mastery.png){ .ab-icon }

#### Arachnid Mutation – Mutation Mastery

</div>

Having spent even more time with mutated D.N.A., your arachnid-based abilities develop further. When your health is below 50%, you gain increased attack knockback in addition to automatically attacking anything within 5 blocks of you.

<div class="chips"><span class="chip">Health <b>Below 50%</b></span><span class="chip">Attack knockback <b>+2</b></span><span class="chip">Auto-attack <b>5 blocks</b></span></div>

</div>

</div>
