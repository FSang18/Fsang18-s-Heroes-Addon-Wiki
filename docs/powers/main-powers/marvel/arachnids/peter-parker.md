The "Arachnid-Peter Parker" power is obtained via 

Stats:

* Maximum Health: 44
* Maximum Healing / Second: 0.2/s
* Armor Rating: 13
* Armor Toughness: 10
* Base Attack Damage: 12.6
* Sprint Speed: 4.3 b/s
* Flight Speed 6.2 b/s

* Spider Sense: Detect any danger within 20 blocks of you. Additionally, player that deal damage to you will trigger your spider sense for up to 10 seconds after they hit you. The direction the danger is coming from will be represented by the 
angle of the color displayed on your screen.

* Arachnid Mobility: Crouch when sprinting to slide, knocking back entities in your path. You can also do a double jump by pressing the jump key while in the air.

* Arachnid Adhesion: By enhance the inter-atomic attractive forces between your skin and a surface, sharply increase friction to stick to/ climb on any surface. Wall Crawling can be toggled on/off with key bind set.

* Arachnid Carry: Using your enhanced strength, and with empty main/off hands, carry the target in front of you.

* Arachnid Dodge: Using your enhanced mobility, dodge in the direction you are moving to avoid incoming damage.

* Arachnid Communication: Due to you being one of the Spider-Totems, connected to the Web of Life and Destiny, all Spiders and Cave Spiders will ignore you.

* Web of Life:  Due to you being one of the Spider-Totems, connected to the Web of Life and Destiny, gain the passive ability to sense any spider, or player with an Arachnid-type power, within 20 blocks of you, even through walls. Additionally, you will be able to walk through Cobwebs without any issue.

* Great Weaver's Favor:  Due to you being one of the Spider-Totems, connected to the Web of Life and Destiny, channel the powers of the Great Weaver to grant all arachnids within 10 blocks (including yourself) Regeneration II for 5 seconds.

* Arachnid Mutation Organic Emergence: Due to extended time with mutated D.N.A. your powers are facing an emergence of newly acquired capabilities. When your health is below 50%, you'll gain an increased +2 attack damage but will also begin to automatically attack anything within 5 blocks of you.

* Organic Web Zip: Due to your mutated D.N.A. gain the ability to use your Organic Webbing to fire a Web-Line at any block pull yourself towards it. Firing at another entity will pull the entity towards yourself instead.

* Organic Web Swinging: Due to your mutated D.N.A. gain the ability to create Organic Webbing to swing from. By default it'll be turned off, but can switch to assisted or freeform swing modes with the "Swing Mode Change" ability.

* Organic Web Shot: Due to your mutated D.N.A. gain the ability to fire off small shots of Organic Webbing, alternating from each arm, to deal low damage and give your target the Webbed Effect III for 5 seconds. Unlike the Web-Shooters, this draws on your own Organic Webbing and costs no Web Fluid.

* Organic Web Impact: Having refined your Organic Webbing further, fire off a larger shot to deal more damage, knock your target back, and give them the Webbed Effect VI for 10 seconds. This draws on your own Organic Webbing and costs no Web Fluid.

* Arachnid Mutation - Arachnid Evolution: Having spent more time with your mutated D.N.A gain an even further development of your arachnid-based abilities. Where in addition to "Arachnid Mutation Organic Emergence" your spider-sense will be much more accurate at telling where entities around your are at the cost of your senses being overloaded when it's activated in your mutated state until mastered.

* Arachnid Mutation - Enhancement:
	* Structural - Your mutation causes your bones to become tougher. Making so the damage you take from falling is now reduced to only 5%.
  * Strength - Your mutation causes your muscles to become tougher, granting a permanent +2 to your attack damage rating.
  * Physical - Your mutation causes your skin to become denser, granting a permanent +4 to your armor rating.
  * Durability - Your mutation causes your skin to tougher, granting a permanent +2 to your armor toughness rating.

* Arachnid Mutation - Mutation Mastery:  Having spent even more time with mutated D.N.A. gain a further development of arachnid- based abilities. When your health is below 50%, you'll gain an increased +2 attack knockback in addition to automatically attack anything within 5 blocks of you.  

* Web Blossom: Having pushed your Arachnid Mutation to its next stage, gain the ability to launch yourself into the air and spin rapidly, unleashing a burst of Organic Webbing in every direction to overwhelm and restrain those around you. Like your other Organic Webbing, this draws on your own Organic Webbing and costs no Web Fluid.

This is one of the few Arachnids that have organic webs the other is Silk
