# Arachnids

Arachnids are individuals who possess spider-like abilities, typically gained through exposure to radioactive or genetically altered spiders. Their powers commonly include enhanced strength, speed, agility, wall-crawling, and a spider-sense that warns them of danger. Depending on the individual, they may also possess unique abilities such as organic webbing, venom blasts, camouflage, or enhanced biological traits.
