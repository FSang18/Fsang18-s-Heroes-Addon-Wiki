# Arachnids

Arachnids are individuals who possess spider-like abilities, typically gained through exposure to radioactive or genetically altered spiders. Their powers commonly include enhanced strength, speed, agility, wall-crawling, and a spider-sense that warns them of danger. Depending on the individual, they may also possess unique abilities such as organic webbing, venom blasts, camouflage, or enhanced biological traits. Here you can find all the information about arachnid powers.

<!-- power-cards -->

## Obtaining an Arachnid Power

- When a radioactive spider attacks you, there is a 5% chance you will gain the **Arachnid Poisoning** effect.
- The effect can be cleared by drinking milk or by dying.
- If it is not cleared, you keep it until you sleep in a bed for the night.
- When you wake up, you will have one of the Arachnid powers, chosen at random.
