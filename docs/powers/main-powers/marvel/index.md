# Marvel

Marvel characters possess a wide range of extraordinary abilities, often gained through genetic mutations, scientific experiments, advanced technology, exposure to radiation, or other supernatural and cosmic phenomena. Their powers vary greatly, including superhuman strength, enhanced senses, energy manipulation, elemental control, and a variety of unique abilities.
