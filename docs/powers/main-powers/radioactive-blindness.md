# Radioactive Blindness

![Radioactive Blindness](../../uploads/770ff9d6ab45ec46afb331b9f354ca46/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Radioactive Blindness"** is obtained via finding a radioactive lake in a swamp biome submerge the player and then after exiting the radioactive lake wait one minute and then they will get this power.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 4.5 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 8
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Block"** - Block incoming damage for a short time.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Enhanced Punch"** - Punch a target, knocking them back. Increasing your strength skill tree increases the damage of this attack.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Disarm Target"** - Disarm the target within 2 blocks of you to make them drop anything they may be holding in their main or off hands.

**Related Information**:

* Your expertise in combat training allows you to easily fight with **both arms** as if they were both your mainhand.
* You are blind with this power.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* You can see entities outlines 60 block away
* This power is based off of [Matthew Murdock, a.k.a Daredevil](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Matthew_Murdock_\(Earth-616\)).
