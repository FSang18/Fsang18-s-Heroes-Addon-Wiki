# Shazam

![Shazam](../../uploads/697c9fafc79d834a60ccb6a5d0cb857e/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"SHAZAM"** is obtained via trading a [Wizard ](../../villagers/wizard.md)villager for a Shazam Staff, then typing "Shazam" into the chat, to say the wizard's name and claim his power.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 56
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 2 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 25
* Armor Toughness: 10
* Base Attack Damage: 15
* Sprint Speed: 850%
* Flight Speed: 1.8

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Blessing of Mercury"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-based Grab"** - With an empty main / offhand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Speed of Mercury Barrage"** - Rapidly punch your target with both hands. This ability scales with your strength output.
  * **"Speed of Mercury Dash Strike"** - Use your speed to instantly close in on your target before delivering a power strike to anything in your way. This ability has a range of 10 blocks.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Blessing of Zeus"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Power of Zeus"** - Use your power to channel The Living Lightning into a beam from your hands.
  * **"Lightning Chain" -** Use your power to channel The Living Lightning to call a strike of lightning at your target which will then chain to the next nearest entity to your target. This can strike up to three targets per use.
  * **"RF Charging"** - Use your power to channel The Living Lightning to charge the RF of any item in your main or offhands.

**Related Information**:

* While transformed type **SHAZAM!** into the chat, scream the name of the wizard that grants your power to unleash a devastating attack of lightning, striking everything, in your vacinity at the cost of being dampening afterwards for 1 full minute.
* This power has following _passives_ while your suit is active;
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Explosion Immunity**
  * **Lightning Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* You cannot wear any armor while your suit is active.
* This power has different suit options it can choose from in the Accessory menu for when they are transformed.
* Typing "Shazam" into the chat will allow you to toggle on/off the suit and gain access to your abilities. If you are underwater, your word will be sent of into a bubble which will trigger your transformation when it reaches air, as long as you are within 30 blocks of it when it pops.
* This power has a color slider in the power menu for changing your costume color.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Billy Batson, a.k.a Shazam](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Shazam_\(Billy_Batson\)).
