# Geokinesis

![Geokinesis](../../../uploads/0fd91c2d501f5cdf658d05122e479bac/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **“Geokinesis”** is obtained via consuming a [Dark Matter Sample](../../../items/dark-matter-sample.md).

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 3
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 5
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Levitation Speed: 1.38

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Earth Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Move Earth" -** Using your Geokinetic powers, move earth-like blocks.
  * **"Earth Spikes"-** Using your Geokinetic powers, create a wall of spiked earth for 10 seconds up to 5 blocks away in the direction you are looking. This ability will damage any entity that gets to close to the spikes.
  * **"Earth Bullets" -** Using your Geokinetic powers, convert one of the earth-type blocks in your main hand to a volley of geokinetic bullets to fire off at your target.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Defensive Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Ground Wall" -** Using your Geokinetic powers, create a wall of Earth in front of you for 5 seconds. The type of block will change based on which dimension / biome you are in.
  * **"Stone Armor" -** Using your Geokinetic powers, cover yourself in a hard layer of stone, lowering your movement speed but increasing your armor and toughness. This ability costs 2 Saturation points to be enabled, then may stay on until disabled. Stats gained from this form
    * **Armor: +6**
    * **Toughness: +6**
    * **Sprint Speed: 105%**
  * **"Seismic Sense" -** By focusing on your connection to ground beneath your feet, see entities that are moving and on the ground within a 60 block radius.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): “Earthquake” -** Using your Geokinetic powers, create an earthquake at your location.

**Related Information**:

* This power only use its abilities on the ground, except for Move Earth ability.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Tara Markov, a.k.a Terra](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Tara_Markov_\(Prime_Earth\)).
