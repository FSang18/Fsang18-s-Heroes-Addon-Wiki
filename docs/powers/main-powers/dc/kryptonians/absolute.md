
The "Kryptonian-Absolute" power is obtained via 

Stats:

* Maximum Health: 60
* Maximum Healing / Second: 0.7/s
* Armor Rating: 9
* Armor Toughness: 1.6
* Base Attack Damage: 8.4
* Sprint Speed: 4.3 b/s
* Flight Speed 6.2 b/s

* Strength-based Carry: Grab a target in range and hold them using one of your powerful arms.

* Telescopic Vison: Focus your eyes to zoom in on distant things. Scroll up to zoom in further and down to zoom back out while it is on.

* Super hearing: With your enhanced hearing, you can heal all entities up to 100 blocks away, unless they are on wool/carpet

* X-Ray Vision: Enable your X-Ray Vision to see entities through walls, however you cannot view them if there is palladium:lead in the way. Scrolling will allow you to change target(s) you are viewing between the following: "All", "Players", "Passive", "Hostile"

* Frost Breath: Release a blast of your super breath which can knockback entities in range, freeze them, and also make the ground you freeze slippery. 

* Heat Vision: Drain your Solar energy to fire off blasts of heat from your eyes. Scrolling while this ability is enabled will allow you to adjust the Output of your beams up to level 5 which will increase damage and melt speed.
