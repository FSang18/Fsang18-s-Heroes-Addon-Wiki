# Kryptonians

Kryptonians are a humanoid extraterrestrial species originating from the planet Krypton. Under a yellow sun, they develop extraordinary abilities, including superhuman strength, speed, flight, enhanced senses, and near-invulnerability. Their powers vary depending on their individual traits, exposure to different solar levels, and the universe they originate from. Here you can find all the variants and information regarding them.
