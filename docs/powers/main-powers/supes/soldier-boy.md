# Soldier Boy

![Soldier Boy](../../../uploads/eb66fa2b57889abc631577f794941f1f/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Soldier Boy"** is obtained through random chance by using the item "Compound V".

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 46
* Maximum Healing/Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 20
* Armor Toughness: 10
* Base Attack Damage: 15
* Sprint Speed: 200%

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot.
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - With an empty main / off hand, use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Military Punch"** - Using your unrestrained strength, and an empty mainhand, hit your target with a powerful strike. This ability scales with your damage output.
  * **"Powerful Charged Leap"** - Use your immense strength to leap in the direction you are looking. Hold down the activation key for up to 5 seconds to leap even further, depending on how long you held it. This ability will have a 5 second cooldown after use.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Radiated Self-Explosive Beam"** - Using your stored energy, create an explosion and fire a strong beam. This beam will heavily deplete your stored energy, but can be used to dampen the powers of anyone hit by it and anyone within 3 blocks of where you stand. You can passively gain energy when at 25% of your Max health or lower.

**Relevant information:**

* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has the following _passives_;
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
  * **Explosion Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power is based off of [Soldier Boy](https://the-boys.fandom.com/wiki/Soldier_Boy).
