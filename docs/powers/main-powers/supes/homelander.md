# Homelander

![Homelander](../../../uploads/f05723a5c97671c52caaaf42cba07aa6/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **“Homelander”** is obtained through random chance by using the item "[**Compound V**](../../../items/compound-v.md)".

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 40
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3.5 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 20
* Armor Toughness: 8
* Base Attack Damage: 15
* Sprint Speed: 225%
* Flight Speed: 1.38

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Unforgiving Punch"** - Using your unrestrained strength, hit your target with a powerful strike. This ability scales with your damage output.
  * **"Dashing Strike" -** Dash in the direction you are facing to strike your target with a power attack. This ability scales with your damage output.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): “X-Ray Vision” -** Using your enhanced eyes, gain the ability to see entities through walls for up to 60 blocks away. Scrolling while the ability is active can allow you to change between being able to see All, Players, Passive and Hostile Entities.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): “Laser Vision” -** Unlock the ability to fire laser beams from your eyes. Scroll for changing the output. Output 1 can be used to cook items in furnace but has a lower range, and Output 2 can be used to mine blocks at the cost of lower damage.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): “Toggle Eyes”** - Toggle on / off your red laser eyes. Its just for the cool look.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Close Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fourth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fourth slots are:
  * **"Disorienting Strike"** - Hit the target in front of you in vital sensory locations, stunning them and disorienting them for 5 seconds.
  * **"Repelling Shockwave"** - Rapidly raise your arms with your enhanced strength to repel everything within 5 blocks of you, and damage anything too close with a small shockwave.
  * **"Violent Rip"** - Grab the target in front of you for 2 seconds, then pull them apart with all your might for a massive attack with a high cooldown.

**Related Information**:

* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
  * **When at 100% flight speed you will launch on the ground with creating an explosion around you** and will **destroy any blocks that will come in your way** while flying. For turning off the **destructive flight**, you just need to fly at not 100% flight speed.
* This power has the following _passives_;
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
  * **Explosion Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power is based off of [Homelander](https://the-boys.fandom.com/wiki/Homelander).
