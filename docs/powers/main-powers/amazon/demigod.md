# Demigod

![Demigod](../../../uploads/3ec48438e902bcfd141ac7082f142bbe/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

**The "Amazon - Demigod"** is obtained via right clicking a [Amazonian Artifact](../../../items/artifacts/amazonian-artifact.md). This will grant you the one of the [Amazonian](index.md) powers at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 4 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 9
* Armor Toughness: 2
* Base Attack Damage: 10
* Sprint Speed: 225%
* Flight Speed: 1.38

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Block"** - Block incoming damage for a short time.

**Related Information:**

* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Gamma Radiation Immunity**
  * **Nausea Immunity**
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* Being the child of one of the great gods, every 10 minutes, you will be granted one of the following effects;
  * "minecraft:luck"
  * "minecraft:saturation"
  * "minecraft:health_boost"
  * "minecraft:haste"
  * "minecraft:strength"
  * "minecraft:speed"
  * "minecraft:resistance"
  * "minecraft:saturation"
  * "minecraft:dolphins_grace"
  * "minecraft:fire_resistance"
  * "minecraft:hero_of_the_village"
  * "minecraft:regeneration"
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Diana of Themyscira, a.k.a Wonder Woman](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Wonder_Woman_\(Diana_Prince\)).
