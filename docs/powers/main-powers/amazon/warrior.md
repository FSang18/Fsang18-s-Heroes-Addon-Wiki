# Warrior

![Warrior](../../../uploads/96f8585c36a29e48143c6ce00e01e103/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Amazon - Warrior"** is obtained via right clicking a [Amazonian Artifact](../../../items/artifacts/amazonian-artifact.md). This will grant you the one of the [Amazonian](index.md) powers at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 6 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 15
* Sprint Speed: 275%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Grab"** - Use your strength pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.

**Related Information:**

* Your expertise in combat training allows you to easily fight with **both arms** as if they were both your mainhand.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has following _passives_:
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Poison Immunity**
  * **Gamma Radiation Immunity**
  * **Nausea Immunity**
* This power is based off of [Race of Warrior Womens who lives in Paradise Island, Amazons](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Amazons).
