# Void

![Void](../../../uploads/69b9f5c4518738b98109b48699500606/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

**"The Void"** is obtained via purchasing the ability named "The Void" from the [Golden Sentry](index.md) power.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: +18
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: +4
* Armor Toughness: +4
* Base Attack Damage: +10
* Sprint Speed: 100%
* Flight Speed: +0.5

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Infini-Tendril Strike"** - Fire off one of your Inifi-Tendrils to repeatedly strike those in front of you for 6 damage per tick with a cooldown of 3 seconds.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Ultimate Intangibility"** - Alter your molecular density to phase through solid objects and entities for 5 seconds, inflicting 4 damage per tick to targets you pass through.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Umbrakinetic Voided Shadows"** - Cover the area around you in a 10x10x10 radius with shadows, causing Nasuea and Blindness to those trapped in the darkness for 10 seconds.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Umbrakinetic Dark Sun"** - Charge up Void energy in your main hand for 3 seconds, then when you release it, cause a strong energy explosion to harm anything in a 5x5x5 radius for 30 damage. This ability has a heavy cooldown.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Voided Umbrakinetic Dark World"** - Charge Void energy for 15 seconds and become immobilized with the weakness effect. Afterwards, gives the Wither II effect to all entities within a 25x25x25 for 30 seconds. This will also cause a large destructive explosion at your location, and has a heavy cooldown of 10 minutes.

**Related Information**:

* This is a unique secondary that only Sentry can use.
* **Unlocking this power restricts the use any** [**Secondary Powers**](../../secondary-powers/index.md) **or Arc Reactors.**
* This upgrade is irreversible without cheats.
* Unlocking this power will permanently mutate the Photokinetic Beam from the [Sentry](index.md) power to become Red rather than Gold and will increase in damage from 12 to 14.
* Unlocking this power will permanently mutate the Photokinetic Shield's color.
* During the nighttime, passively gain an increase of +2 to your strength, +4 to your armor, and +8 to your max health.
