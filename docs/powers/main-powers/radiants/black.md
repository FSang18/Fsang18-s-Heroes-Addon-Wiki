# Radiant - Black

![Radiant - Black](../../../uploads/2d9ccbeea2ecc991e08d9385f6792691/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Radiant - Black"** is obtained via discovering a Radiant Black hole rarely in a plains biome. Breaking this block will replace any powers you may have with the Radiant - Black abilities. There is no limit to the amount of these that can spawn per world.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 22
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 14
* Sprint Speed: 275%
* Flight Speed: 1.75

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Radiant Suit"** - Enabled your transformation into your Radiant Black suit, disable your transformation and enable your Second Form.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Radiant Control Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Radiant Fist"** - Infuse your punches with Radiant energy to deal +4 more damage per hit.
  * **"Radiant Telekinesis"** - Use the power of Radiant to lift any target within 5 blocks of you.
  * **"Radiant Gravity Pulse"** - Send out a radial burst of gravitational force, pushing or pulling nearby enemies.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Increase Gravity"** - Use the power of the Radiant to Increase gravity for entities around you based on their proximity to you. Divides the targets motion by the following amounts / range: 100 / 1-2 | 50 / 2/5 | 20 / 6-8 | 10 / 9-10
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Decrease Gravity"** - Decrease all entities around you in a 10x10x10 radius by giving them the levitation effect.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Radiant Energy Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fourth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fourth slots are:
  * **"Radiant Blast"** - Launch a small blast of your Radiant energy at your target that deals 4 damage.
  * **"Radiant Beam"** - Create a large beam of Radiant energy to fire off at your targets deals 12 damage.
  * **"Radiant Explosion"** - Focus all of your energy to charge up a large explosion of Radiant energy.
* **Slot 5 (Pre Second Form Unlock): "Existence"** - Channel your powers to send yourself to / from Existence
* **Slot 5 (Post Second Form Unlock): "Mini Black Hole"** - Create a miniature black whole which is launched at your target for an initial 30 damage, with a high **destructive** explosion radius after contact. this ability has a 30 second cooldown.

**Related Information:**

* This power **cannot** use any [Secondary Powers](../../secondary-powers/index.md).
* This power **cannot** use any [Arc Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/index.md) higher than the [Model II Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/model-ii.md).
* **This power can breath in space using the Ad Astra Mod**
* Once the ability to travel to Existence has been unlocked, placing then breaking a Wither Rose will someone a Robot which can be fought.
  * This Robot must be defeated via **Melee** methods to unlock the second form.
  * Once defeated, unlocking the "Second Form" ability from the ability menu will automatically be transported to the exact coordinates **in the Overworld** you left from.
* While in your second form lose access to all of your abilities, but gain a constant vibrating appearance and following stats:
  * **Strength: 26**
  * **Armor: 28**
  * **Toughness: 4**
* This power is based off of [Nathan Burnett, a.k.a Radiant Black](https://radiant-black.fandom.com/wiki/Nathan_Burnett).
