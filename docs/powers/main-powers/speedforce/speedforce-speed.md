# Speedforce (Speed)

![Speedforce (Speed)](../../../uploads/7951207c27cb86994731c21268af0b70/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Speedforce (Speed)"** is obtained via a random chance when using "Assorted Chemicals" and getting struck by lightning.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 44
* Maximum Healing / Second: 4 HP / 2 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 3
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3 (4.5 at max speed)
* Sprint Speed: 2150%
* Flight Speed: N / A

Main Abilities:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Speedforce Enable Speed"** - Press to activate your speed. When active, you can hold this button and scroll to increase/decrease your speed. Crouch when enabled to rapidly increase your speed
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Speedforce Disable Speed"** - Crouch for turning off your speed.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "High Speed Punches"** - Punch as quick as you can at your target, this attack will scale to your damage output.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "High Speed Arm Rotation"** - This ability will allow you to rotate your arms rapidly when crouching to either knock targets back or slow your decent. Also this ability will put out flames.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Vibrational Phasing"** - When moving at at least 20% vibrate your molecules fast enough to phase through solid objects for up to 10 seconds. This ability has upgrades whic allow you to phase at least 10% speed with 30 seconds phasing. After vibrate your molecules fast enough 5/3 seconds, you become completely invisible to the naked eye.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Extreme Speed Punch"** - While at 15% or more speed level, move faster than the eye can see towards to the target you are looking at to deliver a strong punch containing all of your velocity.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Wide-Area Speed B**litz" - Move quickly from target to target, hitting them and dealing damage as you do. This attack also applies weakness to efect to the targets.

**Related Information:**

* This power can run on water after unlocking the Water Running ability at minimum 20% Speed/ 10% Speed/ 1% Speed.
* This power can run on up walls or run back down after unlocking the Wall running ability. This power also has upgrades which allows you to run faster at walls. This ability can toggle on/off at power menu.
* You can change your Lightning trail with color slider in the power menu.
* This powers damage output will increase depending on your speed level.
