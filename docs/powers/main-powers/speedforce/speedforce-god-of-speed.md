# Speedforce (God of Speed)

![Speedforce (God of Speed)](../../../uploads/316ac88a5aa1c56301ef923adcc7a810/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Speedster (God of Speed)"** is obtained via purchasing the last ability of[ Speedforce(Avatar](speedforce-avatar.md)) variant.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 44
* Maximum Healing / Second: 4 HP / 2 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 3
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3 (23 at max speed)
* Sprint Speed: 3600%
* Flight Speed: N/A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Speedforce Enable Speed"** - Press to activate your speed. When active, you can hold this button and scroll to increase/decrease your speed. Crouch when enabled to rapidly increase your speed
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Speedforce Disable Speed"** - Crouch for turning off your speed.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "High Speed Punches"** - Punch as quick as you can at your target, this attack will scale to your damage output.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "High Speed Lightning Punches"** - Unlocking this will allow you to utilize your lightning when you strike, extending your range and increasing your damage when crouching.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Vibrational Phasing"** - Vibrate your molecules fast enough to phase through solid objects for up to 30 seconds.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Vibrational Phasing"** - When moving at least 5% crouch to phase yourself and anyone within 2 blocks of you for up to 15 seconds
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Throw Lightning"** - You are passively fast enough to throw the lightning that normally trails behind you. This ability deals 12 damage and applies shock II effect for 5 seconds.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Air Leap"** - By moving at least 20% Speed, gain the ability to the move so fast you are able to step on solid air to push yourself off of. When you purchase this, your Lightning Throw will become a crouch ability when moving at 20% or faster.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Slipstream Stasis Field"** - By moving at at least 70% of your speed, create a stasis field around yourself using Speedforce energy for 10 seconds, significantly slowing down every entity around you in a 10x10x10 radius.

**Related Information:**

* This power can run on water at at least 10% speed.
* This power can run on up walls or run back down. This ability can toggle on/off at power menu.
* This powers damage output will increase depending on your speed level.
* When you activate your Speed you become one with the Speedforce gaining The Speedforce Omnipresence Aura. You can change this aura and Lightning Trail with color slider in the power menu.
