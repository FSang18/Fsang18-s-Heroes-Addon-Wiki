# Reality Warping

![reality_warping.png](../../../uploads/d28fd9955985c8c073eb1ad11e534097/reality_warping.png){ width="89" height="89" loading=lazy }

The **"Reality Warping"** is obtained via [X-Gene](../../../items/x-gene.md) item at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: 1.45

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Telephatic/Telekinetic Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Telekinetic Hold"** - Use your telekinetic powers to move an entity or block within 2 blocks of you that your are looking at. Once active, scroll up or down to move them up to 5 blocks away, or 1 block close.
  * **"Spatial Travel** - **Short Range"** - Teleport yourself up to 30 blocks in the direction you are looking with a one second cooldown.
  * **"Psionic** **Strike"** - Hit your target with a Psychic strike to mental damage to them over time.
  * **"Power Detection"** - Use your Telepathic powers to uncover any / all powers your target possesses.
  * **"Telepathic Mental Cleanse"** - Use your Telepathy to reset all of the abilities from a single target's current power. If they die or remove the effect within 5 minutes, they will not be affected. This ability has a very large cooldown.
  * **"Reality Matter Conversion"** - Change the form of **Iron / Gold / Diamond / Netherite** into various tools. This ability has upgrades for use of mentioned ingots.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Energy Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Energy Manipulation Beam"** - Gain the ability to manipulate your stored energy into a powerful beam that deals 6 damage that goes through armor.
  * **"Energy Manipulation Shield"** - Gain the ability to manipulate raw energy into a powerful Ring-Shaped shield in front of you.
  * **"Energy Manipulation Explosion"** - Gain the ability to manipulate raw energy into a massive explosion of energy around you. This will take 5 seconds to build-up before it can be fired, and will heavily drain your energy.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Dimensional Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to use your abilities in your third slot. You need to hover over the abilities and click them for using it. The Abilities for the third slots are:
  * **"Pocket Reality Storage"** - Gain the ability to open a small portal into your pocket dimension to store your items in your own personal storage chest. This portal will remain open and draining your energy until you close it by activating the ability again from the ability wheel.
  * **"Pocket Reality Travel"** - Gain the ability to fully access your pocket dimension, sending only yourself there and back at will. You will always be returned to the exact location in the Overworld you left from.
  * **"Multiversal Access Storage"** - Gain the ability to tap into the Ender Dimension to access your Ender Storage remotely.
  * **"Multiversal Access Travel"** - Gain the ability to teleport yourself and any other entity within 2 blocks to designated locations, including dimensional travel via dimensional menu.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Reality Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fourth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fourth slots are:
  * **"Powerhouse Mode"** - Gain the ability to activate your PowerHouse mode at will. This will increase your stats moderately and you will induce **Weakness 5** to all other entities in a 3x3x3 radius for 10 seconds. This ability will drain your energy heavily. Strength gained from this mode is depends on your energy level, other stats are flat increase and not tied to your energy level. This ability has upgrades for the stats and for the effect. At max Energy(for strength) and at max upgrade gained stats are:
    * **Attack Damage: +20**
    * **Armor Rating: +15**
    * **Armor Toughness: +8**
    * **Maximum Health: +8**
    * **Knockback: +3**
  * **"Cosmic Dampening Technology"** - Channel the power of reality to cause Tech on the person you are looking at in a 5 block range to malfunction for 10 seconds.
  * **"Cosmic Dampening Speed"** - Channel the power of reality to cause Speedforce on the person you are looking at in a 5 block range to be dampened for 10 seconds.
  * **"Cosmic Dampening Red Sun"** - Channel the power of reality to cause Kryptonian DNA on the person you are looking at in a 5 block range to be dampened for 10 seconds.
  * **"Cosmic Dampening Metaphysical"** - Channel the power of reality to cause Cosmic / Magic users you are looking at in a 5 block range to be dampened for 10 seconds.
  * **"Cosmic Dampening Genetic"** - Channel the power of reality to cause Genetic-based powers on the person you are looking at in a 5 block range to be dampened for 10 seconds.
* **Slot 5 (Crouch): "Cosmic Manipulaiton"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fifth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fifth slots are:
  * **"Cosmic Energy Surge" -** Levitate and hold targets within a 10x10x10 radius, dealing 1 damage per second.
  * **"Cosmic Herald Creation"** - Using your cosmic energy, gift a chosen individual increased stats and a fraction of your power for a period of time. As long as they have energy, they can retain the abilities, however there is no way for them to regain lost energy alone.
  * **"Cosmic Gene Reconstruction"** - Use your cosmic powers to reconstruct someone's DNA. If they can survive for 10 minutes with the effect you grant them, they will be given a random X-Gene Mutation. Looking straight down will allow you to use it on yourself. This ability needs to be **re-purchased** after each use.
  * **"Cosmic Reconstruction(Stored)"** - Use your cosmic powers to reconstruct an empty bottle in your mainhand into an X-Gene mutation which can only produce a low-tier Mutations. After 3 uses, this ability needs to be **re-purchase.**

**Related Information:**

* This power has an energy bar that will deplete as you use your ability’s and will refill passively over time.
  * **The Maximum limit for Reality Energy is 1,000,000.**
  * Your energy will replenish faster when you are in your pocket dimension after purchasing the **Pocket Reality - Energy** ability.
* You have a increased healing in your pocket reality after purchasing the **Pocket Reality - Healing** ability.
* This power can understand any **language** spoken, no matter which multiverse you are in after purchasing the **Multiversal Access - Knowledge** ability.
* This powers one of the two powers that can **travel other dimensions**.
* Combining this power with the [Phoenix Force](../../secondary-powers/phoenix-force.md) grants you faster energy generation, which stacks with your Pocket Dimension boost.
* Copying this ability with [Mimicry ](mimicry.md)grants a version of the Pocket Dimension - Storage ability.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Franklin Richards, a.k.a Powerhouse](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Franklin_Richards_\(Earth-616\)).
