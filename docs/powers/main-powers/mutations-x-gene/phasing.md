# Phasing

![Phasing](../../../uploads/daba5833ae6520354fc1eb66d67e27b1/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Phasing"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Phase"** - Use your mutation to phase through entities and blocks for up to 10/20/30/60/90 seconds. After 4th upgrade gain the ability to no longer have to hold for activation.

**Related Information**:

* While you are phasing, you cannot take damage from others, however you also cannot deal any damage to others.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a passive immunity to suffocation while on the ground.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Katherine Pryde, a.k.a Shadowcat](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Katherine_Pryde_\(Earth-616\)).
