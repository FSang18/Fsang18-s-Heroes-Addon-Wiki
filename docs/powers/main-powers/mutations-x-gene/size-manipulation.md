# Size Manipulation

![Size Manipulation](../../../uploads/d317a73de53b826314761e4cf2dfc6e2/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Size Manipulation"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 5
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Size Up"** - Gain the ability to grow in size. The rate at which you grow can be increased by further upgrading this.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Size Down"** - Gain the ability to shrink in size. The rate at which you shrink can be increased by further upgrading this.

**Related Information**:

* This power allows you to change your size, becoming both larger and smaller. Due to its nature, you do not grow stronger or weaker at these sizes
* This power shrinks in increments of approximately 0.25 and 0.5 blocks, down to a minimum of about 0.25 blocks.
* This power grows in increments of approximately 0.25 and 0.5 blocks, up to a maximum of about 28 blocks.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of the Size Down ability that lets the player become 1 block tall with no sizes in-between.
* This power _loosely_ is based off of [Scott Wright, a.k.a Micromax](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Scott_Wright_\(Earth-616\)).
