# Eyes

![Eyes](../../../uploads/96acec36755b258cb8d4ef77d9504cfa/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Eyes"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Related Information**:

* This power grants **Night Vision** and the ability to see targets up to 100 blocks away, and through walls. It also causes eyes to appear all over the player's skin.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* By unlocking Sensory Focus you will learn to see normally.
* After unlocking the Enlightened Vision you will passively gain the ability to tell when there is danger within 10 blocks of you.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you glowing eyes and a larger radius for your danger sense.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Trevor Hawkins, a.k.a Eye-Boy](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Trevor_Hawkins_\(Earth-616\)).
