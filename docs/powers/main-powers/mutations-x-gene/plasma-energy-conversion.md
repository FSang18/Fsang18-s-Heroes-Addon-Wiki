# Plasma Energy Conversion

![Plasma Energy Conversion](../../../uploads/697a8746ef28f1c3872aa4f183c082fc/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Plasma Energy Conversion"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing/Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 3
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 5
* Sprint Speed: 150%

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Plasma Energy: Chest Beam"** - Fire off the energy stored within you into a powerful chest beam that will drain high amount of your energy when used.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Plasma Energy: Hand Beams"** - Fire off the energy stored within you into powerful hand beams that will drain your energy when used.

**Relevant information:**

* You can passively generate plasma energy from the cosmic energy around you to turn into your attacks. If your energy reserves reach 0, then you will be forced into a dampened state for 10 seconds while you charge energy again. You cannot passively generate energy in the Nether.
  * Additionally, you are unaffected by the energy of **Optic Blast** users.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Alexander Summers, a.k.a Havok](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Alexander_Summers_\(Earth-616\)).
