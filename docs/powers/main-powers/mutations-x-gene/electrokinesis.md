# Electrokinesis

![Electrokinesis](../../../uploads/35e995398fbf2726e7b58a9e812858c1/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Electrokinesis"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 2
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch,Holding Drain Item): "Charge Self"** - Using this ability you can drain energy from items to grant yourself power.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch,Holding Charge Item): "Charge Item"** - Using this ability you can give your energy to whatever you are holding.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch, Empty Handed): "Electrokinetic Telekinesis" -** Use your electricity to lift up any conductive block, or any target. Holding your target will slowly drain your electrokinetic energy. Your lightning will not harm any targets hit.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Electrokinetic Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Electrokinetic" Blast -** Using your electricity fire off a blast of energy at your target, shocking them. This ability will have longer range than your beams, but deals less damage. This attack can hit through multiple targets.
  * **"Electrokinetic Beam" -** Using your electricity fire off a beam of energy at your target, deals 8 damage per hit and shocks them. Using this ability will drain your stored energy.
  * **"Electrokinetic Pulse" -** Using your electricity fire off a pulse of energy around your location, shocking and grant the EMP effect to anything within the range..
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Electrokinetic Dash Strike" -** Transform yourself into raw electricity, dashing in the direction you are looking. While performing this dash, your electricity form can grant the Shock Effect to entities you pass through. You will also be able to pass through solid objects during your dash.

**Related Information**:

* You can passively draw energy from worn Arc Reactors after unlocking the "Electrokinetic Self Sufficiency" ability.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* Phoenix Force grant the player passive energy generation.
* Copying this with Mimicry grants immunity to being shocked.
* This power depends on _Rf_ or _Fe_.
* This power can also charge their energy using redstone dust or blocks.
* This can be generated with mods such as: Simple Generators, Thermal Expansion, Immersive Engineering and more.
* You can change your electricity color in power menu via color slider.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Noriko Ashida, a.k.a Surge](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Noriko_Ashida_\(Earth-616\)).
