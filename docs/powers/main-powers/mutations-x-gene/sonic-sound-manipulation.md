# Sonic Sound Manipulation

![Sonic Sound Manipulation](../../../uploads/10da52d27da8f2dad65b397b94ccbc91/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Sonic Sound Manipulation"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Sonic Scream"** - Scream in a high-pitched sound, harming targets in range for 3/6/8 damage per hit for up to 5/10/20 seconds. This ability also applies Sound Poisoning 2 for 4 seconds. This effect refreshes on every hit. The damage dealt and time you can scream for can both be upgraded. At Upgrade 2, you can unlock the ability to propel yourself upwards by screaming, or crouch and use it to slow your fall.

**Related Information**:

* The sonic nature of this power's abilities well against powers with a vulnerability to certain frequencies, such as Symbiotes, Viltrumites, Kryptonians, Half-Kryptonians, Bone Claws and Lupine.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you a damage of 10 per hit as well as Phoenix flame particles on your scream.
* Copying this ability with Mimicry grants immunity to sonic sounds.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Sean Cassidy, a.k.a Banshee](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Sean_Cassidy_\(Earth-616\)).
