# Telekinesis

![Telekinesis](../../../uploads/81e807ad45e8c686e59b428647e57daf/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Telekinesis"** is obtained via X-Gene item at random chance.

Stats:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: 0.8

Main Abilities:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Telekinetic Hold"** - Use your telekinetic powers to move an entity within 10 blocks of you that are lookin at. This ability can upgradable to reach max 30 range.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Telekinesis Blast"** - Use your telekinetic powers to send blast of energy that deal 3 damage and knockback the target.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Telekinesis Aura"** - Use your telekinetic powers to block yourself from incoming projectiles at the cost of not being able to use your other abilities while it is active.

Related Information:

* This power has following _passives_ when Telekinesis Aura is active;
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* Flight and Telekinesis Aura's color can adjustable in the power menu.
* Using Phoenix Force change aura color to yellow and increase the damage and knockback of the telekinesis blast to 5 damage.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Mutant Jean Grey](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Jean_Grey_\(Earth-616\)).
