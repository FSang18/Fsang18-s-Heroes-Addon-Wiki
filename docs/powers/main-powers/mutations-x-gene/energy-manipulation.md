# Energy Manipulation

![Energy Manipulation](../../../uploads/9acb3e965c194ded1dfe06086a50afa2/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Energy Manipulation"** is obtained via the [X-Gene](../../../items/x-gene.md) item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 36
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 12
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 18
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: 2.5

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Energy Cannon"** - Expel a powerful beam of energy at your target, dealing 10 damage per hit.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Energy Pulse"** - Expel a short blast of energy at your target, dealing 8 damage per hit but requiring less energy.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Energy Sphere"** - Use your energy to create a sphere-like construct around you to halt incoming damage.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Energy Healing"** - Manipulate the energy in your body's composition to heal yourself.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Energy Saturation"** - Manipulate the energy in your body's composition to increase your saturation.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Power Negation"** - Fire a blast of energy which can override your target's mutant abilities for 30 seconds while dealing 6 damage on a hit.

**Related Information**:

* This power has an energy bar that fills when using any of the powers. If this bar is completely filled, all of your abilities will be locked for 30 seconds due to overloading your power.
* This power has the following _passives_;
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* Phoenix Force grants you a higher overload limit.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of the Energy Saturation ability.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Gabriel Summers, a.k.a Vulcan](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Gabriel_Summers_\(Earth-616\)).
