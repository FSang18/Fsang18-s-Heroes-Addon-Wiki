# Self Detonation

![Self Detonation](../../../uploads/afe1ea1246720edb2f9b4c9cf147353b/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Self Detonation"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Charge"** - Compress your energy to build up enough for an explosion. This ability has upgrades that increse your charging speed.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Detonate"** - Use your energy to explode, damaging the area and entities around you. This ability is _highly_ destructive and will destroy blocks in a similar fashion to TNT. This deals anywhere from 10 to 46 damage, depending on a target's proximity to the center of the explosion. The radius varies from 5 blocks at Output 1 to up to 15 at Output 10.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Increase Detonation Output"** - Increase your Detonation Output by 1, up to your highest unlocked output.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Decrease Detonation Output"** - Decrease your Detonation Output by 1, to a minimum of 1.

**Related Information**:

* This power has a energy bar for using its abilities, you can't use Detonate ability while your bar is empty.
* This power has **Explosion Immunity**.
* Having the Phoenix Force grants you passive energy generation.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Eloise Phimister, a.k.a Negasonic Teenage Warhead](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Eloise_Phimister_\(Earth-10005\)). It takes inspiration from her movie counterpart and not her comic self.
