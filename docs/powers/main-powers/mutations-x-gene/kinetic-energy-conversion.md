# Kinetic Energy Conversion

![Kinetic Energy Conversion](../../../uploads/6301741def32fb3b227b143b73405497/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Kinetic Energy Conversion"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 24
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 3
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Charge/Throw"** - The ability to charge an item in your hand with kinetic energy, then throw it to detonate on your target for a small explosion that deals 5 damage. This ability will only be active when you hold something that can charge and throwable in your main hand. (Not every item can be used for this)
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Explosion Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Delayed Explosion" -** When on the ground, charge the spot you are standing at, then have it explode 3 seconds after it is placed for a moderate sized explosion.
  * **"Explosive Leap" -** When on the ground, create a small, non-desctructive, explosion below your feet, propelling yourself in the direction you are looking.
  * **"Explosive Knockback" -** Create a small AOE explosion at your location by charging an item in your **main** and **off hands**. This will take 2 seconds to charge up and use both items.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Explosive Card Storm" -** While holding at least 3 items in your mainhand, rapidly charge, then throw multiple projectiles at once.

**Related Information**:

* You are **not immune** to your own(or any) **explosion**.
* This power will turn your eyes black and red.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is based off of [Remy Lebeau, a.k.a Gambit](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Remy_LeBeau_\(Earth-616\)).
