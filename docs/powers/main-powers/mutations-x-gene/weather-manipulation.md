# Weather Manipulation

![Weather Manipulation](../../../uploads/6b967043de70d6054c43a81fd17ff5fa/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Weather Manipulation"** is obtained via X-gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: 0.58 (0.88 when in rain)

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Toggle Weather"** - This ability either lets you change the weather from Clear to Rain, or vice versa.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Wind Current"** - Create a powerful blast on wind to push your target back and deal 1 damage. (increases to 2 damage and gains a stronger knockback when in rain)
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Channel Lightning"** - Summon a lightning bolt to strike so you can act as a lightning rod and channel that lightning at your target dealing 10 damage. (Increases to 16 damage when in rain)

**Related Information**:

* When combined with the Phoenix Force, "Wind Current" Deals 2 damage and 4 damage when in rain.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Ororo Munroe, a.k.a Storm](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Ororo_Munroe_\(Earth-616\)).
