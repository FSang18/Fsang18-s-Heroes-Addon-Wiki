# Optical Beam

![Optical Beam](../../../uploads/806cb755f59c7628fbaaec5aefcae3d2/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Optical Beam"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 24
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Close your Eyes"** - Hold your eyes shut to prevent the non-stop beams of energy from emerging out of your eyes when you are not wearing something to channel energy.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch, with Ruby-Quartz Headgear): "Ocular Beam" -** When your empty hand raised on any Ruby-Quartz Headgear, fire off a beam of controlled Optical Energy. If looking straight down, you can slow your decent at higher outputs. If crouching, you can destroy blocks at higher outputs.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch, with Ruby-Quartz Headgear): "Ocular Blast" -** When your empty hand raised on any Ruby-Quartz Headgear, fire off a blast of controlled Optical Energy which can go further, and bounce off of walls. The higher the output, the higher the knockback.

**Related Information**:

* This power deals a base damage of 18 when not controlled.
* For controlling your power you need either one of the **Ruby-Quartz Headgear** or Phoenix Force power. With an empty mainhand, and using one of the control methods, raise your arm to access to your controlled abilities.
  * Scrolling while using (Crouch+Scroll while not using abilities) allows you to adjust the output.
  * With controlled methods you have a Overheat bar, the more you use your abilities at higher outputs the more it will fill. If your Overheat bar fills completetly, you will have your powers dampened for 30 seconds.
* Using this power with symbiote will give you access the Ocular Beam ability.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is based off of [Scott Summers, a.k.a Cyclops](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Scott_Summers_\(Earth-616\)).
