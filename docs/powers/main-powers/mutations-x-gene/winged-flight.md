# Winged Flight

![Winged Flight](../../../uploads/2f9758f1baf3f3ee633d178900ba6267/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Winged Flight"** is obtained via X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 27
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 7
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Flight Speed: 1.8

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Wing Attack"** - Using you wings, attack your targets for 1 damage with range, if you have Arch-Angel unlocked, this will deal 10 damage and become a melee ability.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Wing Block"** - Once you've unlock Arch-Angel, you can now block incoming attacks with your wings.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Feather Shot"** - Once you've unlock Arch-Angel, you can now shoot razor sharp feathers at you targets dealing 5 damage and poison.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Sonic Scream"** - Scream in a high-pitched sound and deal damage. This ability also applies Sound Poisoning 2 for 2 seconds. This effect refreshes on every hit.

**Related Information**:

* You can change your color of your wings in Accessories menu.
  * Arch-Angel Transformation will grant you forced gray-blue colored skin and wings.
* The "Arch-Angel Transformation" Costs 24 blocks of [Techno-Organic Metal](../../../blocks/ores/techno-organic-ingot.md).
* Phoenix Force grants you Yellow Burning Wings, this will burn your Techno-Organic Metal and revert you from your Arch-Angel form.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants wings.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Warren Worthington, a.k.a Angel/Archangel.](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Warren_Worthington_III_\(Earth-616\))
