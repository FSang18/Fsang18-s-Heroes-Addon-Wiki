# Teleportation

![Teleportation](../../../uploads/7725e205402838ff9fd9e9a323199269/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Teleportation"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 9
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 2
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Short Range Teleport - Self"** - This allows you to teleport up to 30 blocks in the direction you are looking with a cooldown of 0.5 seconds.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Short Range Teleport - Group" -** You can teleport other entities around you, with you.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): " Short Range Teleport - Swap" -** Teleport to your target and switch places with them up to 30 blocks.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Dimensional Teleport"** - You can now teleport from your current position between the Overworld and the Nether with a 1 minute cooldown.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Saved Teleportation 1"** - You can now teleport to a saved coordinate.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Enable Coordinate Entry 1"** - Save the coordinates of a Location to teleport back to.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Saved Teleportation 2"** - You can now teleport to Second a saved coordinate.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Enable Coordinate Entry 2"** - Save the coordinates of a Second Location to teleport back to.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Saved Teleportation 3"** - You can now teleport to Third a saved coordinate.
* **Slot 5 (Crouch): "Enable Coordinate Entry 3"** - Save the coordinates of a Third location to teleport back to.

**Related Information**:

* This powers final unlock allows you to teleport anywhere in the current dimension, to do so you just need to use the "/FSTP" Command.
* This power has a color variant that will change your eyes and skin. Yellow Eyes and Blue Skin. Gray eyes and Red Skin. The color variant of the power will be random.
  * Commands for changing the color variants can be found on the [Commands](../../../commands.md) page.
* This power is loosely based off of [Kurt Wagner, a.k.a Nightcrawler](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Kurt_Wagner_\(Earth-616\)) and [Azazel](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Azazel_\(Earth-616\)).
