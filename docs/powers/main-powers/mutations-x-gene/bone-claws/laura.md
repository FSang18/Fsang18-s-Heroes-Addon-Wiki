# Laura

![Laura](../../../../uploads/86620a7789b8c4dd806c2b04c6c4b3ab/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Bone Claws - Laura"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance. This variant has 2 outer hand claws, and one claw on each foot.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 Heart / second (0.5 / second with Adamantium Skeleton Infusion)
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 9 (15 with Adamantium Skeleton Infusion)
* Sprint Speed: 200%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Activate Claws"** - Allows you to unsheathe your claws.
* S**lot 1 (Crouch): "Deactivate Claws"** - Allows you to retract your claws.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Block** - With your claw out, hold your arms up to block incoming damage.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Claw Grapple"** - Grab a target in front of you within a 2 block range and deal 2 damage per second (4 with adamantium claws) to them while you hold them with your claws.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Feral Senses"** - Gain the ability to highlight entities within a 10 block radius for up to 10 seconds at a time.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Wall Crawling"** - The ability to toggle on / off your wall crawling capabilities while your claws are extended with this ability.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Hunter's Barrage"** - Rapidly swing the claws on your arms and legs to quickly deal damage to your target based on your current output + a strength 1 effect.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Controlled Feral Rage"** - Send yourself into a violent rage, increasing the damage of all of your abilities by +4, your speed by 0.5, and increasing your healing to an addition 0.5 Heart / Second at the cost of being slightly harder to control due to your screen shaking.

**Related Information**:

* This power has passive **Night Vision.**
* In addition, it can infuse itself with 4 Adamantium Blocks to gain increased damage with at the cost of reduced healing.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you passive burning claws that deal fire damage on hit.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of the Bone Claws ability.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Laura Kinney, a.k.a X-23](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Laura_Kinney_\(Earth-616\)).
