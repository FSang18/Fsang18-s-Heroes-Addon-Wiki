# Daken

![Daken](../../../../uploads/977ed9b39c904ddce6977ea5084a1203/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Bone Claws - Daken"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance. This variant has 2 outer hand claws, and 1 on the inner hand.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 Heart / second
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 11
* Sprint Speed: 200%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Activate Claws"** - Allows you to unsheathe your claws.
* S**lot 1 (Crouch): "Deactivate Claws"** - Allows you to retract your claws.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Block** - With your claw out, hold your arms up to block incoming damage.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Pherokinesis - Scent"** - Using your empathic pherokinetic abilities, mask your scent from hostile mobs around you for up to 10 seconds at a time. This ability has the following upgrade;
  * **"Pherokinesis - Vision"** - Upgrade your Pherokinetic abilities to mask you visually, and double the duration you remain masked.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Feral Senses"** - Gain the ability to highlight entities within a 10 block radius for up to 10 seconds at a time.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Wall Crawling"** - Gain the ability to toggle on / off your wall crawling capabilities while your claws are extended with this ability.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Feral Strike"** - Swing both of your arms to strike for a massive attack dealing your current output + a strength 4 effect modifier with a high cooldown of 1 minute.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Mass Pherokinesis"** - By inducing fear via your pherokinesis, grant the Weakness 5 and Nausea 5 effects to all entities within a 10x10x10 radius for 30 seconds
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch, Phoenix Force): "Phoenix Force Explosive Slash"** - By unlocking the Phoenix Force, your final ability becomes a ranged slash which deals 12 damage and can set your target on fire from a distance and will create an explosion.

**Related Information**:

* This power has passive Night Vision.
* In addition, this variant **cannot** infuse itself with Adamantium.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you passive burning claws that deal fire damage on hit.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of the Bone Claws ability.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of[ Akihiro, a.k.a Daken](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Akihiro_\(Earth-616\)).
