# Logan

![Logan](../../../../uploads/58901ac4f4be3dfa23ea95a28f28e186/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Bone Claws - Logan"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance. This variant has 3 outer hand claws.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 Heart / second (0.5 / second with Adamantium Skeleton Infusion)
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 14 (19 with Adamantium Skeleton Infusion)
* Sprint Speed: 200%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Activate Claws"** - Allows you to unsheathe your claws.
* S**lot 1 (Crouch): "Deactivate Claws"** - Allows you to retract your claws.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Block** - With your claw out, hold your arms up to block incoming damage.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Lunging Attack"** - Leap towards your target, dealing damage to anything you come into contact with based on your current damage output + a strength effect 2 modifier.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Feral Senses"** - Gain the ability to highlight entities within a 10 block radius for up to 10 seconds at a time.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Wall Crawling"** - After unlocking Adamantium Claws, gain the ability to toggle on / off your wall crawling capabilities while your claws are extended with this ability.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Feral Barrage"** - Rapidly swing your claws with feral rage to quickly deal damage equal to your current output + a strength 1 effect modifier to your target.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch, No Adamantium): "Savage Berserk Rage"** - Send yourself into a violent rage, increasing the damage of all of your abilities by +6, and increasing your healing to an addition 1 Heart / Second at the cost of being harder to control due to your screen shaking.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch, Adamantium): "Super-Heated Claws"** - By unlocking the Adamantium Skeleton Infusion, your final ability allows you to Super-Heat your claws, granting you a +2 damage output while active and passively setting entities on fire when you hit them.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch, Phoenix Force): "Phoenix Force Slash"** - By unlocking the Phoenix Force, your final ability becomes a ranged slash which deals 12 damage and can set your target on fire from a distance.

**Related Information**:

* This power has passive **Night Vision**.
* In addition, it can infuse itself with 12 Adamantium Blocks to gain increased damage , Wall-Crawling and an different final ability at the cost of reduced capabilities in water and healing.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you passive burning claws that deal fire damage on hit.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of the Bone Claws ability.
* This power is based off of [James Howlett, a.k.a Wolverine](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/James_Howlett_\(Earth-616\)).
