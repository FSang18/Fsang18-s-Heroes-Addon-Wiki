# Speed

![Speed](../../../uploads/8abf73dca83a28ded3073816975d23ea/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Speed Mutation"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 Heart / 1 Second
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 5
* Sprint Speed: 1100%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Activate Speed"** - Gain the ability to run at speeds far faster than before. Unlike Speedsters, this power's speed can be increased only by sprinting and cannot be locked.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "High Speed Punches"** - Punch as quick as you can at your target, this attack will scale to your damage output.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Arrow Catch"** - When enabled, this ability will allow you to catch arrows in front of you.

**Related Information**:

* This power gives you a after-image trail when your super speed is on.
* This power can run on walls and on water once you purchase those skills.
* This powers damage output will increase depending on your speed level.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force gives your after-image fire particles.
* Copying this ability with Mimicry grants a destroy speed of 3 when holding nothing.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Pietro Maximoff, a.k.a Quicksilver](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Pietro_Maximoff_\(Earth-616\)).
