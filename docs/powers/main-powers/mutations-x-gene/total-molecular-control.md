# Total Molecular Control

![Total Molecular Control](../../../uploads/35d5becb59a94d29b09790bd99ca169e/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Total Molecular Control"** is obtained via [X-Gene](../../../items/x-gene.md) item at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 50
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 2 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 23
* Armor Toughness: 10
* Base Attack Damage: 18
* Sprint Speed: 300%
* Flight Speed: 1.15

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot.
  * **"Strength-Based Grab"** - With an empty main / off hand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
  * **"Telekinesis"** - Gain the ability to use Telekinesis, allowing you to lift and throw entities with your mind. Hold the ability key and scroll to adjust the grab distance.
  * **"Energy** **Shield"** - Gain the ability to project a powerful Energy Shield, blocking incoming damage.
  * **"Psionic Strike"** - Gain the ability to use Telepathy, allowing you to project Psionic bolts at enemies.
  * **"Size Alteration"** - Gain the ability to alter your size, increasing your hitbox and physical strength.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Celestial Energy Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot.
  * **"Energy Blast"** - Gain the ability to project Energy Blasts at enemies.
  * **"Celestial** **Explosion"** - Gain the ability to create a powerful Celestial Explosion on impact.
  * **"Celestial Beam"** - Gain the ability to fire a concentrated Celestial Beam at your target.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Techno Genetic Manipulation" -** Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your third slot.
  * **Techno-Organic Khopesh** - Gain the ability to turn any Techno-Organic Ingot into a Khopesh, or any other tool.
  * **Tech Dampening** - Emit a Techno-Organic field that suppresses the technological abilities of nearby entities.
  * **Genetic Dampening** - Emit a Techno-Organic field that suppresses the genetic powers of nearby mutants.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Celestial Abilities" -** Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fourth slot.
  * **"Celestial Regeneration"** - Gain accelerated Celestial Regeneration, healing wounds at an incredible rate.
  * **"Celestial Teleportain"** - Gain the ability to instantly teleport to a target location using Celestial energy.
  * **"Celestial Portal"** - Gain the ability to open Celestial portals, allowing you to travel vast distances instantly.
  * **"Celestial Energy Transfer"** - Gain the ability to transfer Celestial energy to anything in your mainhand that requires RF.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Gene Manipulation - Horsemen"** - Manipulate the X-genes of nearby entities, temporarily granting them the enhanced abilities of your Horsemen.

**Related Information**:

* To unlock ability slots 3 and on, the techno organic upgrade will have to be purchased with 64 Techno-Organic Blocks. When this upgrade is purchased all physical stats will increase.
  * **Health: +8**
  * **Armor: +6**
  * **Toughness: +4**
  * **Strength: +2**
* This power can understand any **language** spoken, no matter which dimension you are in after purchasing the "**Omnilingualism"** ability.
* This power **cannot** use any [Secondary Powers](../../secondary-powers/index.md).
* This power **cannot** use any [Arc Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/index.md) higher than the [Model I Reactor](../../../items/technology/arc-reactors/model-i.md).
* This power has following _passives_;
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power will change your apperance to a ash-like color and will alter your eyes. This appearance will change more upon infusing yourself with Techno-Organic Metal.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [En Sabah Nur, a.k.a Apocalypse](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/En_Sabah_Nur_\(Earth-616\)).
