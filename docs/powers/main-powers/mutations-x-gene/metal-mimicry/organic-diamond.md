# Organic Diamond

![Organic Diamond](../../../../uploads/115ea475c15018063ae4d15da8972a29/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Metal Mimicry (Organic Diamond)"** is obtained via X-Gene at random chance.

Stats:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 16
* Armor Toughness: 8
* Base Attack Damage: 8
* Sprint Speed: 150%

Main Abilities:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch,Not Transformed): "Diamond Form"** - Activate or deactivate your diamond form. Convert your body into organic diamond, gaining strength and armor as well as toughness.
  * (Crouch use for reversing into human form when transformed)
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch,Transformed): "Block"** - Block incoming damage for up to 30 seconds but disabling your ability to attack.
* **Slot 2(Non-Crouch): "Grab" -** With an empty main / offhand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.

Related Information:

* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is compatible with custom accessories.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you +2 attack damage and increased height.
* This power has following passives while in diamond form;
  * **Psyhic Attack Immunity**
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Emma Frost](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Emma_Frost_\(Earth-616\)).
