# Organic Steel

![Organic Steel](../../../../uploads/4b0c082fd70b279ea6c99045241cfe0b/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Metal Mimicry (Organic Steel)"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 24
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 14
* Sprint Speed: 150% (105% when transformed)

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch,Not Transformed): "Metal Form"** - Activate or deactivate your metal form. Convert your body into organic metal, gaining strength and armor as well as toughness. Having this active grants the Slowness 2 debuff, however.
  * (Crouch use for reversing into human form when transformed)
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch,Transformed): "Block"** - Block incoming damage for up to 30 seconds but disabling your ability to attack.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Combat Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Powerful Strike"** - Strike the target in front of you with an attack that scales to your damage output.
  * **"Strength-Based Grab" -** With an empty main / offhand, use your strength to pick up an entity and hold them in front of you.
    * **Fastball Special** - While holding someone with your Grab ability, Right-Click to throw them with all your strength. This ability will not deal any damage on initial contact, but the target being throw is susceptible to taking fall damage still.

**Related Information**:

* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has the following _passives_ while in metal form;
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
  * **Explosion Damage Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power is compatible with custom accessories.
* Activating Metal Form takes you from 1.875 blocks tall to 2.25 blocks tall.
* While Metal Form is active, you sink faster and move slower in water.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you +2 attack damage and increased height.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of Metal Form.
* This power is based off of [Piotr Rasputin, a.k.a Colossus](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Piotr_Rasputin_\(Earth-616\)).
