# Pyrokinesis

![Pyrokinesis](../../../uploads/04ffbdd82fe7c9ddeaa2dc1ca38d439e/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Pyrokinesis"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Flame Touch"** - Heat the air around your hands to produce fire, giving you Fire Aspect.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrokinetic Fireball"** - Launch a ball of flame at an enemy within range, dealing 8 damage and igniting the ground.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrokinetic Firestream"** - Launch a stream of fire dealing 8 damage per hit and igniting the ground. Upgrading this to Enhanced Pyrokinetic Firestream allows you to shoot under yourself and soar upwards. Crouching causes this to instead slow your falls.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Smelt Ore / Cook Food"** - Cook any vanilla food in your hand, or turn any ore in your hand into its respective ingot.

**Related Information**:

* This power has a energy bar for using its abilities, you can't use any of the abilities while your bar is empty. You can charge your energy bar by crouching while on fire. This will absorb any fire beneath the user, extinguishing it as the user charges their reserves.
* This power has following _passives_;
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
* Having the Phoenix Force grants you passive energy generation.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants immunity to fire.
* This power _loosely_ is based off of [St. John Allerdyce, a.k.a Pyro](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/St._John_Allerdyce_\(Earth-616\)).
