# Chronokinesis

![Chronokinesis](../../../uploads/36617646e4b9dd65cd573fad3f1820c7/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Chronokinesis"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Advance Time of day"** - Hold this button to slowly advance the time of day at the cost of slowly increasing your overload bar. Upgrading it further will increase how much time you can advance by.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Adjust Time Control"** - Hold this button while scrolling either up or down to change the amount of time you increase time by.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Chronokinetic Freeze"** - Cast your chronokinetic abilities on any target you are looking at in a 5 block range, decreasing their time to a stand still.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Increase Target's Time"** - Cast your chronokinetic abilities on any target you are looking at in a 5 block range, increasing their speed.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Chronokinetic Enhancements (Others)"** - Gradually increase the time of any target you are looking at to allow them to move faster.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Chronokinetic Enhancements (Self)"** - Gradually increase the time of yourself to allow yourself to move faster
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Chronokinetic Decay"** - Rapidly age the cells in your target, forcing their body to wither away.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Chronokinetic Area Decay"** - At the cost of rapidly increasing your energy usage, decay all targets within a 5x5x5 radius around yourself.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Chronokinetic Slash"** - Slash through the air with raw chronokinetic energy which can decay any target caught in it's path.

**Related Information**:

* To unlock the "Increase Target's Time" ability, you need to max out your "Advance Time of day" power skill line.
* This power is loosely based off of [Heather Tucker, a.k.a Tempo](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Heather_Tucker_\(Earth-616\)).
