# Regenerative Healing

![Regenerative Healing](../../../uploads/14e88d2d41573631d1198baa8e15e70d/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Regenerative Healing"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 40
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 Heart / 0.25 Second
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Related Information**:

* This power gives you a burnt appearance that can be changed through accessories.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you a max of 1 Heart / 0.125 Seconds, and also removes the burnt appearance.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a healing factor of 1 Heart / 1 Second.
* This power is based off of [Wade Wilson, a.k.a Deadpool](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Wade_Wilson_\(Earth-616\)). Despite not always being a true mutant, he is a mutation in the mod as he is mutant-adjacent.
