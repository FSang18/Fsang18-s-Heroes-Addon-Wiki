# Lupine

![Lupine](../../../uploads/bc1184c60ee61b87d2f1f9d4b62df9f6/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Lupine"** is obtained via the [X-Gene](../../../items/x-gene.md) item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 32
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 Heart / 1 Second
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 16
* Sprint Speed: 200%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Activate Claws"** - Use this to pop your claws, granting you higher unarmed damage and allowing use of your other abilities.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Lunge Attack"** - Leap up to 20 blocks in the direction you are looking, dealing damage to any enemies you hit.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Heightened Senses"** - Focus your senses and gain the ability to see an outline on nearby entities for 8 seconds, even through walls.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch): "Wall-Crawling"** - Toggle this to be able to climb walls while your claws are out.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Blood Bash"** - Swing both of your arms to strike for a massive attack with a high cooldown.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Bloodlust"** - Take damage to increase your bloodlust bar, then succumb to the hunger and increase your stats.

**Related Information**:

* This mutation has a Bloodlust bar attacked to it, which will passively increase as you take damage, maxing out at 500.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you passive burning claws that deal fire damage on hit.
* This power _loosely_ is based off of [Victor Creed, a.k.a Sabretooth](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Victor_Creed_\(Earth-616\)).
