# Triple Face

![Triple Face](../../../uploads/9861fbe8b19885f3fca8320da4de3d2f/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Triple Face"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

Stats:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N/A

Related Information:

* Due to your 3 face everything you say in chat will display 3 times.
* You cant wear any helmets with this power due to your 3 face.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you **3x damage multiplier**.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Steve, a.k.a Ugly John](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Ugly_John_\(Steve\)_\(Earth-616\)).
