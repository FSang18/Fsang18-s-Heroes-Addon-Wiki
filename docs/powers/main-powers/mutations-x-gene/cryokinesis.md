# Cryokinesis

![Cryokinesis](../../../uploads/98807e5bd7721cd729ad27eea756597a/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Cryokinesis"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 34
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 1 Second
* Armor Rating: 12
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 11
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight(Levitation) Speed: 1.25

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Cryokinetic Ice Form/Shape Ice Tools"** - Harden your skin with ice. Crouch and activate this ability to disable your form. Create tools made of ice in your main hand, replacing any currently held items, Holding the keybind allows you to change the shape of the ice tool.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Cryokinetic Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Ice Beam"** - Launch a beam of ice at your target which can freeze them for 5 seconds, or create ice temporarily.
  * **"Ice Blast"** - Launch a blast of ice at your target which can freeze them for 3 seconds, or extinguish Fire/Campfire/Lava blocks.
  * **"Ice Dome"** - Create a dome of ice around you for 10 seconds before it disappears.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Cryokinetic Absolute Zero"** - When transformed into your Ice Form create a large explosion of ice around you so cold that it damages anything that is near it. Crouch to change the output which affects the range/damage, but also increases the overall cooldown before you can use this ability again.

**Related Information**:

* This power is compatible with [custom accessories.](../../../accessory-support.md)
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you a stronger Cryokinetic Ice Beam.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants a version of the Cryokinetic Ice Beam.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Robert Drake, a.k.a Iceman](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Robert_Drake_\(Earth-616\)).
