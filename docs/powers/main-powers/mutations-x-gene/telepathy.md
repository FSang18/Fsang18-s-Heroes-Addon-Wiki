# Telepathy

![Telepathy](../../../uploads/6a7877e3da4a3aebd3856e51b7d399f1/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Telepathy"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Telephatic Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Psionic Strike"** - Hit your target with a Psychic strike to deal damage to them. Wearing Cerebro will increase the range and damage output.
  * **"Telepathic Detection"** - Using your telepathic powers, detect those around you in a 20 block range. Wearing Cerebro increases the range to 50, and gains the option to toggle between all entities only mutants, or only non-mutants.
  * **"Telepathic Pacify"** - Using your telepathic powers, hide your presence from hostile mobs.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Psionic Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Psionic Vertigo"** - Hit your target with a Psychic blast to affect their cognitive functions, slowing them down and making them feel sick.
  * **"Psionic Motion"** - Hit your target with a psychic blast to affect their motion, temporarily halting their movement.
  * **"Mass Psionic Motion"** - Spread your reach to hit multiple targets in a 5x5x5 radius with Psychic energy to affect their motion, temporarily halting their movement.
  * **"Psionic Senses"** - Hit your target with a Psychic blast to affect their sense, temporarily blinding them and affecting their ability to touch other entities.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Psionic Lightning"** - Use your Telepathy to channel the ambient Psionic energy in the air through a target having a physical effect. Wearing Cerebro increases the range and damage output.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Uncontrollable Movements"** - Use your Telepathy to severely tamper with the movements of your target
* **Slot 5 (Crouch): "Master of Telephaty"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your fifth slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the fifth slots are:
  * **"Mindless Attack"** - Use your Telepathy to force your target to mindlessly attack anything they are looking at for 10 seconds.
  * **"Mental Detection"** - Use your Telepathy to uncover any / all powers your target possesses.
  * **"Master of Telepathy: Blank Slate"** - Use your Telepathy to force all targets within 3 blocks to reset all of the abilities from their current power. If they die or remove the effect within 5 minutes, they will not be affected. This ability will need to be re-purchased after each use.

**Related Information:**

* This power has the ability to change what color the psionic abilities are in the power menu.
* If used with Phoenix Force the ability "Psionic Strike" gets an increase in damage and gives Psychic Damage for 6 seconds while Psionic Motion now lasts 20 seconds.
* Copying this ability with Mimicry grants immunity to Psychic Damage.
* This power also cannot function while the user wears any Magneto Helmet, nor will any of these abilities affect anyone else wearing any Magneto Helmets.
* This powers telephatic abilites can be amplifiable by wearing Cerebro.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Charles Xaviar, a.k.a Professor X](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Charles_Xavier_\(Earth-616\)).
