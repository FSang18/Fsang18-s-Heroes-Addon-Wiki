# Invisibility

![Invisibility](../../../uploads/b502b91f5cefe0635cbfd3c447978475/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Invisibility"** is obtained via the X-Gene item at random chance.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Invisibility"** - Use your mutant gifts to become invisible. Total time you can stay invisible are 30 seconds / 1 Minute / 5 Minute / 10 Minute and after unlocking last ability indefinite.

**Related Information**:

* Combining this power with the Phoenix Force grants you increased speed while invisible.
* Copying it with Mimicry grants the ability to turn invisible while crouching with an empty hand.
* The Invisibility Mutation grants the ability to become completely invisible, including your armor. Upgrading these skills can increase the time you are able to stay invisible for, and allow you to toggle the ability rather than having to concentrate and hold it.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Alisa Tager, a.k.a Cipher](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Alisa_Tager_\(Earth-616\)).
