# Boogie Woogie

![Boogie Woogie](../../../uploads/c48601c02ea96e4eba6ee1b22b4da78f/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **“Boogie Woogie”** is obtained via killing a Stray and it has a chance to drop a Cursed Totem.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 32
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 9
* Sprint Speed: 160%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): “Boogie Woogie - Single Target” -** Activate the ability then, Right-Click to clap your hands together, and switch places instantly with whatever single target you are looking at. Left-Click to do a fake clap that will not do anything. You will not be able to interact with anything while the ability is toggled on.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): “Boogie Woogie - Random Target” -** Clap your hands together, and switch places instantly with a random target within a 20 block radius.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): “Cursed Body Enhancement” -** Drain your cursed energy reserves to enhance your Armor, Toughness, Attack and Healing based on the amount of cursed energy you currently possess.

**Related Information**:

* This ability has another perk that adds the following:
  * **“Black Flash”** - Once purchased, this passive ability grants a 5%, chance with each hit you make to activate Black Flash for 1 second which will allow deal x2 your current damage output on your next hit.
  * This ability has an energy bar that will deplete as you use your ability’s and will refill over time.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is loosely based off of [Aoi Todo, from Jujutsu Kaijen Universe](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Aoi_Todo).
