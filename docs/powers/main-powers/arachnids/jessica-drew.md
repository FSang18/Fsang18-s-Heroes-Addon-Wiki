# Jessica Drew

![Jessica Drew](../../../uploads/f4fbaea39ee7dd8b934ce812c54515ec/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Arachnid - Jessica Drew"** variant is obtained via random chance from injecting yourself with Radioactive Spider Serum.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 5 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 4
* Sprint Speed: 120%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Reflexive Dodge"** - When your spider-sense is active, manually leap in the direction you are looking to avoid damage.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump and wall crawl. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Venom Blast"** - This is the ability that will allow you to deal 2 damage and administer shock damage for 4 seconds to whoever you touch while it is active.

**Related Information**:

* This power can unlock the ability to glide via the PantheonSent Glide system by crouching in the air.
* This power is based off of [Jessica Drew, a.k.a Spider-Woman](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Jessica_Drew_\(Earth-616\)).
