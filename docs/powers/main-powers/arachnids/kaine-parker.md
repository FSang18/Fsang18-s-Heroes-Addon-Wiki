# Kaine Parker

![Kaine Parker](../../../uploads/cbd0b38b1664ebf32ebe59c5d07c4c93/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Arachnid - Kaine Parker"** variant is obtained via random chance from injecting yourself with Radioactive Spider Serum.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 28
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 5 seconds
* Armor Rating: 8
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 10
* Sprint Speed: 110%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Reflexive Dodge"** - When your spider-sense is active, manually leap in the direction you are looking to avoid damage.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump and wall crawl. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Sting of Kaine"** - Unveil your stingers on the insides of your arms to deal an extra damage4 damage per hit.

**Related Information**:

* The "Mark of Kaine" ability can be toggled on/off via the ability menu after purchase and will passively deal 0.5 damage and apply wither for 2 seconds to any target you are looking at that you are in physical contact with.
  * This ability, when toggled on, will only activate if the wall crawling is enabled.
* This power is based off of [Kaine Parker, a.k.a Scarlet Spider](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Kaine_Parker_\(Earth-616\)).
