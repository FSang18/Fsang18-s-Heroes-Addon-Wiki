# Miguel O'Hara

![Miguel O'Hara](../../../uploads/6baede3f6459bc35b59886ce3bb51928/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Arachnid - Miguel O'Hara"** variant is obtained via random chance from injecting yourself with Radioactive Spider Serum.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 5 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 10
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 10
* Sprint Speed: 105%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Activate Talons"** - Elongate your talons to deal an extra +5 damage.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump and wall crawl. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.

**Related Information**:

* This is the only Arachnid variant without a spider-sense.
* When wearing the Spiderman (2099) Chestplate, the talons will mimic the red color of the suit.
* This power is based off of [Miguel O'Hara, a.k.a Spider-Man 2099](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Miguel_O%27Hara_\(Earth-2099\)).
