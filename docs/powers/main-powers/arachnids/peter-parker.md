# Peter Parker

![Peter Parker](../../../uploads/a07de9bf394b8b2de9a49ce1759679c8/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Arachnid - Peter Parker**" variant is obtained via random chance from injecting yourself with Radioactive Spider Serum.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 10
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 10
* Sprint Speed: 115%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Reflexive Dodge"** - When your spider-sense is active, manually leap in the direction you are looking to avoid damage.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump and wall crawl. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Organic Webbing"** - This will allow you to gain the ability to use Organic Webbing to fire a web shot that deals 4.5 damage.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Organic Webbing - Swing/Zip"** - Gain the ability to use Organic Webbing to Swing / Zip. When you toggle the mode on, holding Right-Click will allow you to Web Swing, and pressing Left-Click will allow you to Web Zip.

**Related Information**:

* This is one of the only 2 Arachnid power to have a form of Organic Webbing.
* This power is based off of [Peter Benjamin Parker, a.k.a Spider-Man](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Parker_\(Earth-616\)).
