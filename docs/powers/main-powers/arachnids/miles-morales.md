# Miles Morales

![Miles Morales](../../../uploads/d9b8f9b6f092a8249715d11e68e8866a/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Arachnid - Miles Morales**" variant is obtained via random chance from injecting yourself with Radioactive Spider Serum.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: 1 HP / 5 seconds
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 6
* Sprint Speed: 115%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Reflexive Dodge"** - When your spider-sense is active, manually leap in the direction you are looking to avoid damage.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump and wall crawl. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Camouflage"** - This is the ability that will allow you to blend in to your environment for a up to 30 seconds.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Venom Blast"** - Deliver a shock to a target within your reach. This will deal an initial 4 damage per hit, and also apply the shock effect for 2 seconds to the target. This attack has a very short range. While active, this will drain 1 Bioelectric energy per tick.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Venom Beam"** - Generate a large amount of Bioelectricity to launch at your target for 8 shock damage per hit. This ability has a moderate range and will drain 3 bioelectric energy per tick.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Venom Blade" -** Hold the stored Venom energy into a blade in your mainhand that deals +7 damage.

**Related Information**:

* This variant has a bioelectricity energy bar which will passively refill over time.
* This power is based off of [Miles Gonzalo Morales, a.k.a Spider-Man.](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Miles_Morales_\(Earth-1610\))
