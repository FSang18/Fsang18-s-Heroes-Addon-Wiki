# Cindy Moon

![Cindy Moon](../../../uploads/f4d42b7adaf2ba01ba7c192050139892/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Arachnid - Cindy Moon"** variant is obtained via random chance from injecting yourself with **Radioactive Spider Serum**.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 26
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 4 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 4
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 6
* Sprint Speed: 130%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Reflexive Dodge"** - When your spider-sense is active, manually leap in the direction you are looking to avoid damage.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Toggle Movements"** - Toggle on/off your super jump and wall crawl. While your super jump is on, crouch+jump for super jump.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Organic Webbing"** - This will allow you to gain the ability to use Organic Webbing to fire a web shot that deals 4.5 damage.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Web Claws"** - Coat your fingers in webbing in the form of claws to deal an additional +3 damage.

**Related Information**:

* This is one of the only 2 Arachnid power to have a form of Organic Webbing.
* This power is based off of [Cindy Moon, a.k.a Silk.](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Cindy_Moon_\(Earth-616\))
