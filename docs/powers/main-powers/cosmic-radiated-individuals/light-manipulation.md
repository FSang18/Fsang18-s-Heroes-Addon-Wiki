# Light Manipulation

![Light Manipulation](../../../uploads/a7f500ba02be567b4a4eef713a1bc268/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Light Manipulation"** is obtained via right-clicking a [Galactic Cluster Sample](../../../items/galactic-cluster-sample.md) in the End and waiting for a Cosmic Storm to strike you and up to four people.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 32
* Maximum Healing / Second: N/A
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N/A
* Base Attack Damage: 3
* Sprint Speed: 150%
* Levitation Speed: 1.38

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Light Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot.
  * **"Light Manipulation: Invisibility"**- Manipulate ambient cosmic energy to bend light around you, making yourself invisible. This ability will give the Invisiblity effect for 2 second. This ability is toggleable.
  * **"Light Manipulation: Invisible Air"**- Manipulate ambient cosmic energy to bend light around you, making yourself and others around you in a 3x3x3 radius invisible. In return your movement speed will be heavily reduced. This ability can be used up to 10 seconds.
  * **"Light Manipulation: Blindness"** - Manipulate ambient cosmic energy to turn optic retinas invisible, causing blindness to targets hit. This ability gives target Blindness II and Darkness II for 5 second.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Psionic Abilities"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot.
  * **"Psionic Hyperspace Force Field"** - Through concentration, project an invisible field of psionic force drawn from Hyperspace. This shield will block all incoming damage, but lower your mobility when enabled.
  * **"Psionic Hyperspace Shockwave"** - Use your Force Field energy directed towards an entity to hit them with a heavy force, and knock them back as well.
  * **"Psionic Hyperspace Telekinesis"** - Through concentration, project an invisible field of psionic force drawn form Hyperspace to lift and manipulate entities. Scroll up or down while active to change the grab range.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Energy-based Internal Damage"** - Create Force fields inside a target to deal -4 of internal damage which bypasses armor, per second. While this ability is enabled, your movement will be heavily reduced and you will not be able to use any of your 2nd slot abilities.

**Relevant information:**

* When 50% or less of Max Health, you will gain the ability to detect all negative karma-based entities within 20 blocks. The range become 30 if blinded.
* When invisible your armor will be hidden but your shadow will not.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power is _loosely_ based off of [Susan Storm, a.k.a Invisible Woman](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Susan_Storm_\(Earth-616\)).
