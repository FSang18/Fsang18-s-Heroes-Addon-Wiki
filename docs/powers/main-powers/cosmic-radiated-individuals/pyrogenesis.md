# Pyrogenesis

![Pyrogenesis](../../../uploads/799f3fd0a4c2249435cc8a38b10a3f2f/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Pyrogenesis"** is obtained via right-clicking a [Galactic Cluster Sample](../../../items/galactic-cluster-sample.md) in the End and waiting for a Cosmic Storm to strike you and up to four people.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: 30
* Maximum Healing/Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 10
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 11
* Sprint Speed: 180%
* Flight Speed: 1.38

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrogenetic Form Control/Pyrogenetic Flame Hands"** - Open the ability wheel to select if Pyrogenetic Flame Hands, and Pyrogenetic Form Control are enabled or disabled.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrogenesis: Flaming Meteor"** - Dash in the direction you are looking igniting things around you with your high temperature, and dealing damage to any entity that gets too close.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrogenesis: Flaming Stream"** - While fully in flames, shoot a large beam of flames at your target. Scroll while firing to change the output of your flames at the cost of overheating quicker. If at max output, you no longer smelt blocks, but instead break any non-fireproof blocks.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrogenesis: Flame Tornado"** - Create a Flaming Tornado up to 10 blocks away in the direction you are looking. Any entity within 5 blocks will be tracked by the tornado and take fire damage based on proximity to the tornado. After 15 seconds, the tornado will extinguish itself.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Pyrogenesis: Super Nova"** - Increase the temperature of your body to burn away the surrounding area. Scrolling while active can raise your temperature, damage output and range at the cost of increasing your overheat bar.

**Relevant information:**

* This power has an "Overheat" bar attached to it. When this is full, you will be unable to use your powers for up to 30 seconds.
  * When underwater, or in rain, your overheat bar will increase slower.
* **_WARNING:_** Any armor that is not fireproof will burn when you fully transform. The list below are armors that are safe to wear;
  * Netherite armor
  * Fantastic Four Armor (Base)
  * Any Future Foundation Armor (Base)
  * God Emperor Doom Armor
  * Savitar Armor
  * Any Uru-Based Armor
* This power can have it's abilities toggled on / off by typing "Flame On" / "Flame Off" in the chat.
* You can decrease / increase your Sprint Speed and Flight Speed with the correct keybinds. Default settings are Down Arrow / Up Arrow. Crouch using will get your percentage to maximum or minimum.
* This power has the following _passives_ while Flame On;
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Lava Damage Immunity**
  * **Freezing Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
  * **TACZ Bullet Immunity Compatibility**
* This power is _loosely_ based of off [Johnny Storm, a.k.a Human Torch](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Jonathan_Storm_\(Earth-616\)).
