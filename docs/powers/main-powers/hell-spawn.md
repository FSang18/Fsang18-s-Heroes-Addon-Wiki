# Hell Spawn

![Hell Spawn](../../uploads/770ce21f3572254494bd559db1ecb2c5/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Hellspawn"** is obtained via the hellfire pact which can be crafted by surrounding 1 piece of paper with 8 hellfire souls (obtainable from killing wither skeletons as a rare drop) in a crafting table.

![Hell Spawn](../../uploads/932cc4c29e982558f285b1d5dfbbe3a2/image.png){ width=253 height=130 loading=lazy }

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: 34
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 3 Seconds
* Armor Rating: 24
* Armor Toughness: 4
* Base Attack Damage: 12
* Sprint Speed: 125%
* Flight Speed: 0.65

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Suit Up!"** - This allows you to enable / disable the Hellspawn suit.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Cape Storage"** - Wear [Hellspawn Cape](../../suitsets/cosmetic/capes/hellspawn-cape.md) the crouch to gain access to your ender chest storage.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Summon Agony Axe"** - With an empty mainhand, summon the Agony Axe to use as a weapon.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Necroplasmic Telekinesis"** - Manipulate your Necroplasmic energy to lift entites and blocks.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Necroplasm Beam"** - Shoot a beam of Necroplasm Energy at your target that deals 8 damage per hit.
* **Slot 4 (Crouch): "Hallucikinesis Aura"** - Confuse those around you, casting them into blind darkness, give them Nausea, Darkness, Blindnes and Weakness for 15 seconds.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Divine Mode"** - Gain access to your Divine Mode and gain an increase to your stats, and faster flight. If your Karma falls below -1,000, you will no longer be able to access this form.
* **Slot 5 (Crouch): "Power Bestowal"** - For 30 seconds at a time, temporarily grant your target a fraction of your power of Hellspawn. This is a crouch ability unless your karms falls lower than -1,000.

**Related Information**:

* This power has following _passives_ while your suit is active;
  * **Night Vision**
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Fire Immunity**
  * **Arrow Immunity**
* You cannot wear any armor while your suit is active.
* This power has an energy bar that will deplete as you use your ability’s and will refill when you **kill hostile mobs.** The energy gaining will be increase when you upgrade the appropriate skills. While in the Nether your energy passively recharges.
* **WARNING:** If your energy reaches 0, you will be instantly transported to the Nether.
* The Maximum limit of the **Necroplasmic Energy is 10,000.**
* When in **Divine Form** you will gain the following stat bonuses:
  * **Health: +8**
  * **Damage: +4**
  * **Armor: +6**
  * **Toughness: +8**
  * **Flight Speed: +0.25**
* This power will gain additional stat bonuses the less karma you get. You will gain the maximum stats at -10,000.
  * **The bonus stats gained from max negative Karma's are:**
    * **Health: +16**
    * **Damage: +8**
    * **Armor: +8**
* This power is based off of [Hellspawn](https://imagecomics.fandom.com/wiki/Hellspawn).
