The “_Riot_” Symbiote is one of the random Symbiotes that can be obtained as a secondary power.

![Riot.png](../../../../uploads/c00eba8b75ecd0357a7a6cd46fafac7a/Riot.png){ width=139 height=218 loading=lazy }

Riot’s arm weapon.

This Symbiote has the unique ability:

- (Non-Crouch) **Riot Camouflage**: Turn Invisible for up to 1 minute at a time, and gain +4 to your armor toughness while invisible.
