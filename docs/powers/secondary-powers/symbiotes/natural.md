# Natural

![Natural](../../../uploads/ede824ffb97c77aebbc302bdd57a6794/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Natural Symbiote"** is obtained via finding a rare crashed meteor above ground in a Plains biome, and killing the Symbiote that has a 30% chance to spawn from breaking the Mysterious Block in the center.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: +6
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 4 Seconds
* Armor Rating: +10
* Armor Toughness: +6
* Base Attack Damage: +7
* Sprint Speed: +50%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Suit up"** - Activate your symbiote suit to gain the abilities of the symbiote who you are a host of.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Anti-Suit up"** - Change into your anti-form, allowing this suit to not retreat when you are on fire, increasing your healing to 2 HP / 2 Seconds, gaining +2 to your attack damage, gaining an immunity to the wither, poison and nausea effects, but losing access to your other symbiote abilities while in this form.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Arm Weapon"** - Change your right arm into a weapon which varies in appearance based on the symbiote you are the host of, but will always grant a +6 attack damage when in use. This ability can be used even without being fully transformed. Check each Symbiote's wiki entry for more details on what their weapon looks like.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Symbiote Tendrils"** - Have your symbiote explode its tendrils in all directions around you to deal 5 damage in a 2x2x2 radius around you.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Signature Power"** - This ability varies based on the symbiote you are a host to. Check each Symbiote's wiki entry for more details.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Shield"** - Increase the mass on your arms to block incoming damage for up to 10 seconds.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Spawn"** - Ability allows the user create a symbiote spawn to create a new natural symbiote for another player. Killing this Symbiote Spawn will grant the killer a new symbiote. After each use, this ability will need to be re-purchased to be used again.

**Related Information**:

* **Inner Codex Bond:** The codex which develops as you grow your bond with your Symbiote. Foster this connection to unlock further abilities and control over your Symbiote, and their cooperation.

  > **_WARNING:_** You need to activate the symbiote talk ability by typing your symbiotes name in chat, then typing Mask, Legs or Wall Crawl. If you don't type your symbiote name and send it, the commands wont work. 
  * **Symbiotic Bond - Activate:** Type the name of your symbiote into the chat to talk with it. You can give commands after activating your symbiote talk.
  * **Symbiote Bond - Mask:** After initiating contact with your symbiote, type "**Mask**" into the chat while your full suit is de-activated for your symbiote to cover only your face and grants you **Night Vision.**
  * **Symbiote Bond - Legs:** After initiating contact with your symbiote, type "**Legs**" into the chat while your full suit is de-activated for your symbiote to cover only your legs and grants you **Enchanced Speed.**
  * **Inner Codex - Wall-Crawl:** By being bonded while in possession of an **Arachnid** variant, grant your symbiote the ability to climb walls, permanently. Once unlocked, type "**Climb**" into the chat with your symbiote listening.
  * **Inned Codex - Symbiotic Bond:** Your bond with this symbiote will continue to grow stronger over time. Every day you remain bonded the same Symbiote, you will gain an increase to your stats, when transformed.
    * The stats gained will remain even if you die, unless you remove your symbiote.
    * **Max Codex Bond Day is 300.**
    * Stat additions below are are based on Max Codex Day:
      * **Damage: +10**
      * **Armor :+10**
* The Symbiote Bond Ability allows the Symbiote to protect the host when they are low on health in exchange for some of their food bar.
  * If the food bar is too low, the ability will not automatically activate.
* All Symbiotes are available as Natural Symbiotes.
