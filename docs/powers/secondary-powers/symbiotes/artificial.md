# Artificial

![Artificial](../../../uploads/59386210f5416d2b1523f5d3462f5ab7/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The "**Artificial Symbiote"** can be created by combining Augmented Human DNA, a Wither Skull, and a Phantom Membrane in a crafting table. Right-clicking this "Artificial Symbiote" will grant a symbiote that doesn't have the full personality of a Natural symbiote, and cannot create a Symbiote Spawn.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: +4
* Maximum Healing / Second: 2 HP / 4 Seconds
* Armor Rating: +14
* Armor Toughness: +4
* Base Attack Damage: +5
* Sprint Speed: +50%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**in Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Suit up"** - activate your symbiote suit to gain the abilities of the symbiote who you are a host of.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Anti-Suit up"** - Change into your anti-form, allowing this suit to not retreat when you are on fire, increasing your healing to 1 Heart / 2 seconds, gaining +2 to your attack damage, gaining an immunity to the wither, poison and nausea effects, but losing access to your other symbiote abilities while in this form.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Arm Weapon"** - Change your right arm into a weapon which varies in appearance based on the symbiote you are the host of, but will always grant a +6 attack damage when in use. This ability can be used even without being fully transformed. Check each Symbiote's wiki entry for more details on what their weapon looks like.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Symbiote Tendrils"** - Have your symbiote explode its tendrils in all directions around you to deal 5 damage in a 2x2x2 radius around you.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Signature Power"** - This ability varies based on the symbiote you are a host to. Check each Symbiote's wiki entry for more details.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiote Grasp"** - Plunge your Symbiote's Tendrils into the ground, having them come out and grab any entity in a 5x5x5 radius, dealing 0.5 damage per tick and immobilizing them, but draining your Fury Meter.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch): "Symbiotic Fury"** - Revert your symbiote to its most primal form, reverting it to a form of pure power and lack of identity. to gain +9 to your attack damage, but at the cost of rapidly drains your Fury Meter by 10 points per tick.

**Related Information**:

* This secondary power has a "Symbiotic Rage" bar on the side of the ability bar which maxes out at 1,000.
  * This bar can be filled by taking any damage while a host of an Artificial symbiote.
