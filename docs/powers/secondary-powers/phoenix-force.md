# Phoenix Force

![Phoenix Force](../../uploads/15dc2e10499fbc69c80fc8161fdd7334/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Phoenix Force"** is obtained via a 1% chance after using a **Totem of Undying** in the End while holding a **Phoenix Force Totem** in the offhand while having a mutant power active. This Secondary power can only be used by [Mutants](../main-powers/mutations-x-gene/index.md), and can be stored within a Phoenix Force Totem.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: +8
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: +6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: +4
* Sprint Speed: +10%
* Flight Speed: +0.8

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Cosmic Pyrokinesis"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your first slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the first slots are:
  * **"Concussive Force Blast"** - Fire off the blast of Concussive Force from your hands to push your target back. (Can be upgraded to double the damage but also double the cooldown)
  * **"Resurrection Pyrokinesis"** - Fire off a beam of your Cosmic Pyrokinesis that channels the Resurrection Force to heal your targets rather than damage them.
  * **"Cosmic Pyrokinesis"** - Fire off the cosmic power of the Phoenix Force from your hands, setting entities on fire. (Can be upgraded to add two outputs which increase damage and heat at the cost of range and accuracy. At max heat, it will smelt blocks around it on contact and evaporate water.
* **Slot 2 (Crouch): "Cosmic Manipulation"** - Open the ability wheel to change what active ability you have for your second slot. Selected abilities are used with non-crouch. The Abilities for the second slots are:
  * **"Disintegration Wave"** - Fire off a wave of Disintegration Force around you in a 3x3x3 radius using the power of the Phoenix Force, harming all of those who are too close, and knocking them back
  * **"Psionic Nexus"** - Use your ability as the Nexus of all Psionic powers to dampen any Genetic-based powers within a 10 block radius for 30 seconds. This ability has a very long cooldown
  * **"Resurrection Wave"** - Surround the area around you in a 5x5x5 radius, healing everything in that range, including yourself, using the power of the Resurrection Force. While active, you cannot move or deal damage.

**Related Information**:

* This power has custom glowing eyes and an aura when flying.
  * The aura / wings particles can be toggled via the ability menu.
* By unlocking the skill named "**Phoenix Force - Reborn**" you will be able to be brought back to life once per purchase and granted healing when it occurs.
  * This skill cannot be stacked to gain more than 1 extra life at a time, and will need to be repurchased for 12 Phoenix Force Token after each use.
* If you are not a mutant, this power will automatically be stored in a Phoenix Force Totem.
