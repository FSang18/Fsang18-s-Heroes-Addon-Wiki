# No. 8

![No. 8](../../../uploads/812cd7361c615880f11e1a08849de76a/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

The **"Kaiju No. 8"** is obtained via Killing a Kaiju. A kaiju will have a 5% chance to spawn anywhere / anytime a Silverfish spawns.

**Stats**:

* Maximum Health: +6
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: +16
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: +6
* Sprint Speed: +50%
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Transformation"** - Change into one of the various forms as they are progressively unlocked.
* **Slot 1 (Crouch): "Change Form"** - Change between the Full and Partial Transformations.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Energy Roar"** - Roar to release a powerful beam of energy which deals 8 (15 once mastered) damage per hit for a long range at the cost of 3 (then 6) energy per tick.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Energy Boost"** - Increase the following stats by the given values at the cost of 2 energy per tick being trained for up to 30 seconds. Attack: +2 | Movement Speed: +0.2
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Berserk Form"** - at the cost of 50 energy per second, you can grow in size by +2.0 and gain the following stat changes for up to 30 seconds: Movement: 0.2 | Attack +6 | Armor +6 |

**Related Information**:

* The Kaiju operates on an energy system.
  * Being Fully transformed drains more energy than being partially transformed
  * Not being transformed can regain the expended energy.
* After purchasing the "**Kaiju Healing**" ability, when your health reaches 2 or less hearts, and you have at least 4 bars of food left, you will be healed and have your foodbar drained.
* Before unlocking the "Controlled Berserk Form" ability, you will automatically transform into the berserk mode if you take too much damage for 30 seconds.
* This powered can be stored in a [Empty Syringe](../../../items/empty-syringe.md)
