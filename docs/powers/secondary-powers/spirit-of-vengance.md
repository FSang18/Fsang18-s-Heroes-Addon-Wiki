# Spirit of Vengance

![Spirit of Vengance](../../uploads/649463d305a0dc16533551f5fe490883/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

The **"Spirit of Vengance"** is obtained via [Unholy Pact](../../items/artifacts/unholy-pact.md). Killing villagers in the nether has a 5% chance of dropping a Unholy Pact, typing "I accept" while holding the pact grants the power.

**Stats:**

* Maximum Health: +8
* Maximum Healing / Second: N / A
* Armor Rating: 6
* Armor Toughness: N / A
* Base Attack Damage: 5
* Sprint Speed: +10% (350% with Hellfire Bike)
* Flight Speed: N / A

**Main Abilities:**

* **Slot 1 (While not transformed): "Transform"** - Transform into the Ghost Rider form and access your abilites.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Hellfire Tools"** - While holding the correct tools you can transform them into the Hellfire version. The transformable tools / items are Chain / Sword / Pickaxe / Axe / Hoe / Shovel / Minecart. Hellfire tools naturally function like they have fire aspect and this tools can also cook blocks that are mined.
* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "GET OVER HERE!"** - Placing Hellfire Chain in your **"curios:hand"** you can use this chains while empty-handed to grab entities towards yourself and burn them. If you use chains on blocks it will pull you towards those blocks.
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Hellfire Blast"** - Channel Hellfire to burn your targets. At output 1, fire it from a single hand and deal 4 damage. At output 2, fire it from both hands and deal 8 damage. At output 3, fire it from your mouth, deal 8 damage and evaporate water / ice blocks when hit. Using this ability at max output will eat away your **saturation.**
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Penance Stare"** - Target the sins in your target within 2 blocks to deal damage them, slow them and weaken them. Their Karma rating will directly tie into the damage taken as will the drain of their Karma points. This ability causes you to lose 3 Karma each second when used.

**Related Information:**

* After unlocking the **Sin Perception** ability you will passively gain the ability to have all entities within a 15 block radius of you with negative Karma highlighted, even through walls.
* Once unlocked **The King of Hell** you will get a crown which grants you the ability to no longer be **killed outside of the Nether.** Instead of dying, when your HP is 1 or less, you are immediately sent to the Nether. Additionaly, you will gain a **passive healing factor** whenever you are in the Nether. You can toggle on / off the crown in the ability menu, toggling of the crown means you dont get the benefit from its abilities.
* With transforming a minecart you will get a **Hellfire Bike** and a massive speed increase, you will create a fire trail wherever you go. The Hellfire bike will stack with your speed increases from other sources.
* This power can be stored with a [Empty Contract](../../items/empty-contract.md)
* You cant wear any helmets while you are transformed.
* You have the following passives while in Ghost Rider Form;
  * **Fire Damage Immunity**
  * **Fall Damage Immunity**
  * **Hunger Damage Immunity**
* The Hellfire tools that created by you will burn other players (who are not Spirit of Vengance users) when they have this tools in their inventory.
* You will transform automatically at nights until you buy the ability of **"Controlled Transformation"**.
