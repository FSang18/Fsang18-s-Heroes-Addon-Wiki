# Neptune's Trident

![Neptune's Trident](../../uploads/3e185ceaef699905f030763e55c7f879/image.png){ width=94 height=298 loading=lazy }

**"Neptune's Trident"** is obtained via crafting the trident with x1 Nth Template, Vanilla Trident and 1x [Nth Block](../../blocks/ores/nth-metal.md) inside a smithing table.

![Neptune's Trident](../../uploads/5052108aa949ea56dcdadeebac60592c/image.png){ width=302 height=67 loading=lazy }

* Swim Speed: 250% 
* Flight Speed: N/A

**Main Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch): "Trident Block / Leap"** - Guard with your Trident in front of you to block all incoming damages for a short time. Right-Clicking while blocking allows you to leap in the direction you are looking. 
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Pressurized Water Stream"** - Shoot a steady stream of water from your trident. This ability can be used to dowse flames, campfires and lava. If you are underwater or rain, this ability will do x2.5 damage.
* **Slot 3 (Non-Crouch): "Weather Change"** - Change to weather to rain if the sky is clear, or clear if it is raining.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Oceanic Orb of Neptune"** - Create an orb of water around you and anyone within a 5x5x5 radius, but you will not be able to move while this ability is active. This ability can only be used by one of the [Atlantean ](../main-powers/atlanteans/index.md)variants.

**Related Information**:  

* This tertiary power uses Nth templates to unlock new abilities.
* By crouching and left clicking you can throw the Trident. Once thrown, it will summon a lightning strike where it lands. You can turn it off the thrown in the power menu.
* While wielding the power of Neptune's Trident, you will gain strength.
* While wielding the Trident, you will be able to swim faster.
* This Tertiary has special interactions when used by one of the [Atlanteans](../main-powers/atlanteans/index.md).
  * Strength and Swim Speed gained from the Trident will be higher.
  * You will deal more damage with **Water Stream** ability.
