# Trails

<p class="argon-intro">Trails follow an entity as it moves. These are the trail types Argon adds.</p>

<p class="argon-count">1 listed &middot; Argon 1.0.0</p>

### Motion Blur Trail {#argonmotion_blur}

<div class="argon-meta"><span class="argon-meta-label">ID</span><code class="argon-id" title="Click to copy">argon:motion_blur</code></div>

<div class="argon-desc">A short, translucent, tapering ribbon built from the entity's recent positions that reads as a motion blur, with optional thin secondary streaks and surface dust. It follows the entity's actual speed: nothing is drawn below minimum_trail_speed, and length, opacity and width grow smoothly towards maximum_trail_speed. Use with tick_delay 1 and a lifetime of at least max_age.</div>
