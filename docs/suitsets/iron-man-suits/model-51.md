# Model LI

![Model LI](../../uploads/3c2e7905c4e7176e71b7d30c580fe0c3/image.png){ width="202" height="374" loading=lazy }

The **Iron Man (Model LI)** is crafted in the Tailoring Table with x3 Gold Circuits, x3 Diamond Circuits, a Template II suitset, x5 Red Reinforced Fabric, x5 Green Reinforced Fabric, x5 Blue Reinforced Fabric, and the Iron Man Model LI blueprint. The armor can be repaired in an anvil with [Modular Stark Repair Kits](../../items/technology/stark-repair-kits/2-enhanced-stark-repair-kit.md).

**Stats**:

* **Armor Rating:** 28
* **Armor Toughness:** 8
* **Attack Damage bonus:** +2
* **Flight Speed:** 1.6
* **Fall Resistance:** 51

**Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch)**: **"Repulsor Beam"** - Fire a continuous beam of repulsor energy that deals damage on hit that can be held for 10 seconds before needing to recharge. This armor has an adjustable repulsor damage output up to a max of 4 that can be adjusted by crouching and using the ability key:
  * Output 1 - 5 damage
  * Output 2 - 8 damage
  * Output 3 - 13 damage
  * Output 4 - 16 damage
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Unibeam"** - Fire a continuous beam of repulsor energy from your chest reactor that deals damage 18 on hit that can be held down for 5 seconds before needing to recharge.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch):** **"Swap Munitions"** - Crouching and using the ability key gives you the option to change the type of munition that will be fired:
  * **"Kinetic Micro Missile"** - Fire a medium range missile that explodes on impact, dealing 3 explosive damage.
  * **"Concussive Blast"** - fire off a burst of concussive wind which inflicts the target with Sound Poisoning for 4 seconds which applies Slowness II, Nausea II, and 2 sound damage over time.
  * **"Taser Launcher"** - Fire a single electric charge which inflicts Shock II and EMP, for 5 seconds which deals 2 shock damage over time and disables the target’s technology based powers.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch):** **"Weapon Changer"** - crouching and using the ability key gives you the option to transform the weapon on this ability.
  * **"Disable Tools"** - retract the Nanites used to form the currently active tool
  * **"Hand Cannon"** - Form your suit’s gauntlets into a cannon to buff your repulsor damage by 5.
  * **"Energy Blade"** - Focus repulsor energy into a blade of pure energy coming from the repulsor core on your gauntlet, giving you 10 damage per hit at the cost of high energy drain.
* **Slot 5 (Non-Crouch):** **"Enable Stealth Mode"** - turn your suit completely invisible while standing still, draining energy whilst active and will disengage any hostile mob tracking you. Moving around will render you only partially invisible.

Additional Information:

* This armor’s primary and secondary colors are able to be recolored via the power menu.
* This armor has complete immunity to sound damage.
* This Suits's recipe becomes unlocked after crafting the Model L suit.
