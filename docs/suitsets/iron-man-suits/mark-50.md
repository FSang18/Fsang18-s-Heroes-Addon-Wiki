# Mark L

![Mark L](../../uploads/c2459632dce42759bfe263dc6fa16dd4/image.png){ width="202" height="376" loading=lazy }

The **Iron Man (Mark L)** is crafted in the Multiversal Iterator with an Earth-199999 Multiversal Extrapolator and the [Model L Endo-Sym Housing Unit](model-50.md). The armor can be repaired automatically by refilling Nanites while transformed or in a smithing table with an Vibranium Smithing Template and an Iron Circuit.

**Stats**:

* **Armor Rating:** 28
* **Armor Toughness:** 8
* **Attack Damage bonus:** +2
* **Flight Speed:** 1.75
* **Fall Resistance:** 61

**Abilities**:

* **Slot 1 (Non-Crouch)**: **"Repulsor Beam"** - Fire a continuous beam of repulsor energy that deals damage on hit that can be held for 10 seconds before needing to recharge. This armor has an adjustable repulsor damage output up to a max of 4 that can be adjusted by crouching and using the ability key:
  * Output 1 - 5 damage
  * Output 2 - 8 damage
  * Output 3 - 13 damage
  * Output 4 - 16 damage
* **Slot 2 (Non-Crouch): "Unibeam"** - Fire a continuous beam of repulsor energy from your chest reactor that deals damage 18 on hit that can be held down for 5 seconds before needing to recharge.
* **Slot 3 (Crouch):** **"Swap Munitions"** - Crouching and using the ability key gives you the option to change the type of munition that will be fired:
  * **"Kinetic Micro Missile"** - Fire a medium range missile that explodes on impact, dealing 3 explosive damage.
  * **"Concussive Blast"** - fire off a burst of concussive wind which inflicts the target with Sound Poisoning for 4 seconds which applies Slowness II, Nausea II, and 2 sound damage over time.
  * **"Taser Launcher"** - Fire a single electric charge which inflicts Shock II and EMP, for 5 seconds which deals 2 shock damage over time and disables the target’s technology based powers.
* **Slot 4 (Non-Crouch): "Tool Selection" -** crouching and using the ability key gives you the option to transform the weapon on this ability at the cost of Nanites:
  * **"Disable Tools"** - retract the tools of your armor back into the housing unit.
  * **"Energy Blades"** - Form your suit’s gauntlets into quick swinging energy blades that deal 9 damage.
  * **"Hand Cannon"** - Form your suit’s gauntlets into a cannon to buff your repulsor damage by 5.
  * **"Cluster Cannon" -** Form some nanites into four floating cannons behind you that buff your repulsor damage by 10.
  * **"Mallets" -** Form your suit’s gauntlets into slow swinging mallets that deal 15 damage. These mallets also have the ability to mine blocks up to stone.

**Related Information**

* This Suits's recipe becomes unlocked after crafting the Model L suit.
