# Moon Knight (Black, White & Blood)

![Moon Knight (Black, White & Blood)](../../uploads/a1f1b62719999d00e152ee430beffa1a/image.png){ width="196" height="367" loading=lazy }

**"Moon Knight (Black, White & Blood) Suit"** is obtained via:

* x2 Blue Tri-Polymer Fabrics
* x2 White Tri-Polymer Fabrics
* Diamond Armor Set

in a Tailoring Bench and can be repaired via "Reinforced Fabric Repair Kits".

**Stats**:

* Armor Rating: 20
* Armor Toughness: 4
