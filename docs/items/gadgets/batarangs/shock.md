# Shock

![Shock](../../../uploads/fc1ceefb3fbd1f165d84ef6dc732d446/image.png){ width=100 height=100 loading=lazy }

**Shock Batarangs** can be obtained via crafting with 1x Copper ingot, 1x [Iron Circuit](../../technology/circuits/iron-circuit.md), and x2 [Batarangs (Normal) ](normal.md)in a crafting table, yielding x4 Shock Batarangs.

![Shock](../../../uploads/2a10f4019393b592cdb12a1034d78f18/image.png){ width=287 height=142 loading=lazy }

Uses:

* Shock Batarangs can be thrown, dealing 5 damage on impact, additional 4 Shock damage over time, and applying EMP 2 and Shock 2 for 5 seconds.
* Up to x16 of these can be stored in any Utility Belt
