# Phoenix Force Totem

**Phoenix Force Totem** has 2 variant, filled totem meaning its store the Phoenix Force power, empty totem can be crafted by crafting with x4 [Cosmic Ingot](../items/material/cosmic-ingot.md), x4 Fire Charge and x1 Totem of Undying inside a crafting table.

<img width="493" height="228" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/51ec0537-6a89-436c-9429-9c8279edd9b2" />

Uses:

* This totem can be used to obtain [Phoenix Force](../../powers/secondary-powers/phoenix-force.md) power.
* Right-Clicking a Phoenix Force Totem (Empty) while having Phoenix Force Power, will remove the power and store it inside.
