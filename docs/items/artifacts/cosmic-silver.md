# Cosmic Silver

<img width="100" height="100" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bffb1971-b112-4dd1-a1c7-eb9348c6033b" /> <img width="100" height="100" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b5e6f9b-de66-4505-8c4a-a7acad51a15f" />

**Cosmic Silver** can be obtained via finding them, it has a rare chance of appearing in End City chests. Its rare as the rarest smithing template that can be obtaianable in end city chests.

Uses:

* Right-Clicking a Cosmic Silver (Filled) will grant you the [Power Cosmic](../../powers/secondary-powers/cosmic.md).
* Right-Clicking a Cosmic Silver (Empty) while having [Power Cosmic](../../powers/secondary-powers/cosmic.md), will remove the power and store it inside the Cosmic Silver.
