# Multiversal Extrapolator

![Multiversal Extrapolator](../../uploads/bc115965dd3ffedec7c62dba950b61b1/image.png){ width="100" height="100" loading=lazy }

**Multiversal Extrapolator** is obtained via finding them inside a chest at Multiversal Structure. This is part of base Palladium.

Uses:

- By Right-Clicking you will get a random universe. You can use this to obtain different multiversel variant of [Armorsets](../../suitsets/armorsets/index.md).
- Some Main Powers have increased chances to get certain Extrapolator Universe's.
- For transforming the suits you need a Multiversal Iterator Block.

Related Information:

- You can duplicate a Multiverse Extrapolator Universe by putting 2 of them and a [Quartz Circuit ](circuits/quartz-circuit.md)in a crafting table.
- You can clear Multiversal Extrapolator from its dimension to basic one by putting it in a crafting table with a [Quartz Circuit](circuits/quartz-circuit.md).
- You can craft another Multiversal Extrapolator by x1 Multiversal Extrapolator, x1 [Vibranium Circuit](circuits/vibranium-circuit.md) and x7 Diamond inside a crafting table.
