The "**Scourge Virus**" can be obtained via brewing Potion of Weakness with a [Viltrumite Fossil](items/artifacts/fossils/viltrumite-fossil.md) inside a Brewing Stand.
<img width="287" height="158" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32d860ac-7332-4da9-9066-c43c010b1de3" />

Uses:

* When used on any [Viltrumite](powers/main-powers/viltrumites) variant it starts to rapidly drain their hunger (0.5 saturation point every second), after their saturation run out it starts to give them 5 Void Damage every second.
* When used on Full Blooded Viltrumite variants it gives Slownes 4 and Weakness 4 effect in the duration of Scourge Virus effect.
* When used on [Viltrumite - Human Hybrid](powers/main-powers/viltrumites) variant it gives Slownes and Weakness effect in the duration of Scourge Virus effect.
* When used on [Viltrumite - Thraxan Hybrid](powers/main-powers/viltrumites/human-hybrid.md) variant it only drains hunger and gives damage afterwards, no slowness and weakness, in the duration of Scourge Virus effect.

Related Information:

*  When under the effect as a Viltrumite you can not hold a Milk Bucket.
*  Scourge Virus has no effect on Non-Viltrumites.
*  Scourge Virus has 4 different variaton for giving the effect
      * Potion of Scourge Virus
      * Splash Potion of Scourge Virus
      * Lingering Potion of Scourge Virus
      * Arrow of Scourge Virus
