## Using it

This is where you insert the vials, be it empty or full.

- **To equip a power:** put its vial into the plus square and close the door of the machine. Hit Start when ready.
- **To remove a power:** do the same steps, but with an empty vial.

The DNA Transfer Station also shows:

- The power you currently have equipped.
- A green bar that shows the power stored in the multiblock.

## Access modes

<div class="blk-modes">

</div>

## Use in the DNA Recoding Chamber

Place the DNA Transfer Station on top of the [Power Block](power-block.md), and put the [Chamber](chamber.md) next to them, to build the [DNA Recoding Chamber](dna-recoding-chamber.md).
