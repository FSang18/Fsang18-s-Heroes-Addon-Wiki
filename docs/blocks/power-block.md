## Uses

- The Power Block is one of the three blocks needed to equip and unequip powers with the [DNA Recoding Chamber](dna-recoding-chamber.md).
- Its max charge is **100,000**.
- It can also be used to charge other RF items.

<div class="power-notes" markdown>

!!! warning "Breaking It"
    When the Power Block is broken, the power stored in it is **not** saved.

</div>
