# Karma

Karma can be gained or lost via killing mobs in your world. It can be raised or lowered nearly infinitely. The specific mob you kill is tied to a specific value as listed below:

## Passive Mobs

Killing the following passive mobs will grant -1 Karma when killed:

| minecraft:cow | minecraft:sheep | minecraft:pig | minecraft:chicken |
|---------------|-----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| minecraft:rabbit | minecraft:horse | minecraft:donkey | minecraft:mule |
| minecraft:camel | minecraft:cat | minecraft:fox | minecraft:parrot |
| minecraft:frog | minecraft:goat | minecraft:axolotl | minecraft:sniffer |
| minecraft:squid | minecraft:glow_squid | minecraft:cod | minecraft:salmon |
| minecraft:bee | minecraft:dolphin | minecraft:wolf | minecraft:mooshroom |
| minecraft:llama | minecraft:turtle | minecraft:bat | minecraft:tropical_fish |

## Positive Mobs

Killing the following positive mobs will remove the corresponding Karma when killed:

| minecraft:villager | **-50** |
|--------------------|---------|
| minecraft:iron_golem | **-50** |
| minecraft:wandering_trader | **-50** |
| minecraft:player | **-100** |
