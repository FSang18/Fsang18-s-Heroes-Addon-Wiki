    Arachnid – Peter Parker

    Main Power · Marvel · Arachnids Arachnid – Peter Parker
    Power ranking B B Tier
    SourceBiologicalGenetic-based power, powered by mutated D.N.A.
    Power classificationFighterMobilitySensoryCrowd Control
    Obtained fromFILL INFILL IN
    Ability slots12345Spider-Totem · Movement · Physiology · Webbing · Mutation
    Signature lookOrganic WebbingWeb lines, shots and more

    Based on Peter Parker, aka Spider-Man. A spider-themed power built on enhanced mobility, wall-crawling and a spider sense that warns of danger. As your mutation grows you gain Organic Webbing, which costs no Web Fluid, and a set of stronger arachnid abilities.

    Stats

    Maximum Health44
    Maximum Healing / Second0.2/s
    Armor Rating13
    Armor Toughness10
    Base Attack Damage12.6
    Sprint Speed4.3 b/s
    Flight Speed6.2 b/s

    Abilities

    Spider-Totem Arachnid Movement Arachnid Physiology Organic Webbing Arachnid Mutation

    Spider-Totem

    Being one of the Spider-Totems connects you to the Web of Life and Destiny.

    Spider Sense

    Detect any danger within 20 blocks of you. Players that deal damage to you also trigger your spider sense for up to 10 seconds after they hit you. The direction the danger is coming from is shown by the angle of the color displayed on your screen.

    Range 20 blocksTrigger on hit 10 seconds

    Web of Life

    Passively sense any spider, or player with an Arachnid-type power, within 20 blocks of you, even through walls. You can also walk through Cobwebs without any issue.

    Range 20 blocksPassive Always on

    Great Weaver's Favor

    Channel the powers of the Great Weaver to grant all arachnids within 10 blocks (including yourself) Regeneration II.

    Range 10 blocksEffect Regeneration IIDuration 5 seconds

    Arachnid Movement

    Enhanced mobility for getting around quickly and climbing anything.

    Arachnid Mobility

    Crouch while sprinting to slide, knocking back entities in your path. You can also double jump by pressing the jump key while in the air.

    Slide Crouch while sprintingDouble jump Jump in air

    Arachnid Adhesion

    By enhancing the inter-atomic attractive forces between your skin and a surface, you sharply increase friction to stick to and climb any surface. Wall Crawling can be toggled on and off with a key bind.

    Toggle Key bind

    Arachnid Dodge

    Using your enhanced mobility, dodge in the direction you are moving to avoid incoming damage.

    Direction Where you move

    Arachnid Physiology

    Your mutated body makes you stronger and tougher.

    Arachnid Carry

    Using your enhanced strength, and with empty main and off hands, carry the target in front of you.

    Requires Empty hands

    Arachnid Mutation – Enhancement (Structural)

    Your mutation causes your bones to become tougher, so the damage you take from falling is reduced to only 5%.

    Fall damage 5%

    Arachnid Mutation – Enhancement (Strength)

    Your mutation causes your muscles to become tougher.

    Attack damage +2 (permanent)

    Arachnid Mutation – Enhancement (Physical)

    Your mutation causes your skin to become denser.

    Armor rating +4 (permanent)

    Arachnid Mutation – Enhancement (Durability)

    Your mutation causes your skin to become tougher.

    Armor toughness +2 (permanent)

    Organic Webbing

    Webbing produced by your own body. It draws on your Organic Webbing and costs no Web Fluid. This is one of the few Arachnid powers with organic webs, the other being Silk.

    Organic Web Zip

    Fire a Web-Line at any block to pull yourself towards it. Firing at another entity pulls that entity towards you instead.

    Block Pulls youEntity Pulls it to you

    Organic Web Swinging

    Create Organic Webbing to swing from. It is turned off by default, and you can switch to the assisted or freeform swing modes with the "Swing Mode Change" ability.

    Default OffModes Assisted · Freeform

    Organic Web Shot

    Fire small shots of Organic Webbing, alternating from each arm, to deal low damage and give your target the Webbed effect. Unlike the Web-Shooters, this costs no Web Fluid.

    Damage LowEffect Webbed IIIDuration 5 seconds

    Organic Web Impact

    Having refined your Organic Webbing further, fire a larger shot to deal more damage, knock your target back, and give them the Webbed effect. Costs no Web Fluid.

    Damage HigherKnockback YesEffect Webbed VIDuration 10 seconds

    Web Blossom

    Having pushed your Arachnid Mutation to its next stage, launch yourself into the air and spin rapidly, unleashing a burst of Organic Webbing in every direction to overwhelm and restrain those around you. Costs no Web Fluid.

    Launch Into the airBurst Every direction

    Arachnid Mutation

    Extended time with mutated D.N.A. unlocks newly acquired capabilities.

    Arachnid Communication

    Because you are one of the Spider-Totems, connected to the Web of Life and Destiny, all Spiders and Cave Spiders will ignore you.

    Affects Spiders · Cave Spiders

    Arachnid Mutation – Organic Emergence

    Your powers are facing an emergence of newly acquired capabilities. When your health is below 50%, you gain increased attack damage but also begin to automatically attack anything within 5 blocks of you.

    Health Below 50%Attack damage +2Auto-attack 5 blocks

    Arachnid Mutation – Arachnid Evolution

    Your arachnid-based abilities develop further. In addition to Organic Emergence, your spider sense becomes much more accurate at telling where entities around you are, at the cost of your senses being overloaded when it is activated in your mutated state, until mastered.

    Spider Sense More accurateDrawback Sensory overload until mastered

    Arachnid Mutation – Mutation Mastery

    Having spent even more time with mutated D.N.A., your arachnid-based abilities develop further. When your health is below 50%, you gain increased attack knockback in addition to automatically attacking anything within 5 blocks of you.

    Health Below 50%Attack knockback +2Auto-attack 5 blocks

    Relevant Information

    • This is one of the few Arachnids that have organic webs. The other is Silk.
    • This power is based on Peter Parker, aka Spider-Man.
