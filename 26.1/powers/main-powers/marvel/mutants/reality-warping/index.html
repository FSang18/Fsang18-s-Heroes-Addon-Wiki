    Reality Warping

    Main Power · Marvel · Mutants Reality Warping
    Power ranking S S Tier
    Source Biological Powers that are genetic-based. Reality Warping is unlocked through the X-Gene.
    Power typeOffenseDefenseManipulationUtilityMobility
    Obtained fromX-GeneRandom Chance
    Ability slots12345Psionic · Energy · Spatial · Reality · Cosmic
    Signature lookBlue EnergyBeams, Projectiles and more

    Reality Warping is an extraordinary power that allows the user to manipulate and reshape reality, drawing on psionic, energy, spatial, reality, and cosmic powers. It grants abilities such as telepathy, telekinesis, energy projection, dimensional travel, matter alteration, and environmental transformation, making it an extremely versatile power with both offensive and defensive abilities.

    Abilities

    Reality Warping has five ability slots. Jump to one:

    Psionic Manipulation Energy Manipulation Spatial Manipulation Reality Manipulation Cosmic Manipulation

    Psionic Manipulation

    Use your Telephaty or Telekinesis via various methods.

    Psionic Strike

    Directly attack a target's mind within 6 blocks. This will deal 4 Psionic damage to the target when hit.

    Range 6 blocksDamage 4 Psionic

    Psionic Read

    Use your telepathic reach to reveal the information of Entities around you in a 30 block radius. If you are using Cerebro to enhance your abilities, you will also be able to read any powers targets in range have.

    Radius 30 blocks

    Psionic Sight

    Use your telepathic reach to see through another target's eyes. You will be able to hop up to 3 times into different targets.

    Hops up to 3

    Telekinetic Hold

    Telekinetically grab any entity or block within 8 blocks of you. Scrolling while this is enabled will allow you to bring the target closer or further.

    Range 8 blocks

    Telekinetic Repulsion

    Telekinetically repel all targets within 3 blocks of you away from you.

    Radius 3 blocks

    Telekinetic Barrage

    Lift, then launch up to 5 blocks within a 6 block radius of you at your target for 4 damage per hit.

    Blocks up to 5Radius 6 blocksDamage 4 per hit

    Energy Manipulation

    Use your power to manipulate different energies in various ways.

    Reality Conversion (Multiversal)

    Dip into your ability to rewrite reality to convert any item in your mainhand into its multiversal counterpart, if it has any.

    Reality Conversion (Store)

    Use some of your energy to register items within your mental database. This will consume the item being registered, and will not retain any enchantments / nbt data. Items entered in the Argon mod's config file blacklist cannot be saved for creation.

    Reality Conversion (Create)

    Use some of your energy to create items within your mental database. Once registered, each item has a max of 5 times it can be created before needing to be re-registered in your mental database.

    Creates per registration 5

    Energy Beam

    Manipulate your energy to fire off a beam of raw reality energy.

    Energy Shield

    Manipulate your energy to create a shield of raw reality energy in front of you. With this shield, you will be able to block up to 50 damage in front of you before it breaks.

    Blocks up to 50 damage

    Energy Explosion

    Manipulate the energy inside you by charging it up for 5 seconds, then exploding it out of you harming everything within a 5 block radius of you.

    Charge 5 secondsRadius 5 blocks

    Spatial Manipulation

    Use your power to manipulate the space around you, and gain mastery over it.

    Spatial Travel (Short)

    Fold the distance between you and a location up to 15 blocks in the direction you are looking to appear there instantly.

    Distance up to 15 blocks

    Pocket Dimension: Storage

    Tap into the power of your Pocket Dimension to store up to 32 items in a controlled space that only you have access to.

    Capacity 32 items

    Pocket Dimension: Travel

    Create a small portal to send yourself to your Pocket Dimension. Using this ability while in the Pocket Dimension will return you to where you were when you left.

    Spatial Travel (Multiversal)

    Gain the ability to open a menu to create portals that allow you and others to travel throughout different dimensions.

    Reality Manipulation

    Use your power to shape and manipulate the reality around you.

    Reconstruction

    Using the item(s) in your mainhand, convert it into anything that uses it as a crafting material. You will need the amount of said item used in that attempted item's crafting recipe to reconstruct it.

    State Changer

    Alter the reality of a targeted block to change the blockstate of any block you are looking at between any of its available states.

    Compact

    Select an area to alter into a compact item. Placing the item on a block will return it to its full size.

    Biome Shift

    Transform reality around you in a 12 block radius to that of a random biome. Those who are caught in the dome with you will not be able to escape / break any blocks until the ability is released. Based on the biome you changed to, have the following effects granted to all within the altered reality:

    Radius 12 blocks
    Plains RegenerationDesert HungerSnowy Taiga FreezeDripstone Caves Fire resistanceDeep Dark BlindnessNether Wastes BurningSoul Sand Valley WitherBasalt Deltas WeaknessEnd Barrens Saturation

    Cosmic Manipulation

    Use your power to manipulate the Power Cosmic around you.

    Cosmic Conduit

    Enter a meditative state to passively pull in Cosmic energy from around you to rapidly regenerate your reality energy bar, and create a light source around you. You will not be able to move, and your flight speed will be reduced by 90% while this ability is enabled, and you will also take 2x damage from all sources.

    Flight speed -90%Damage taken 2x

    Cosmic Herald

    At the cost of a significant amount of your stored energy, grant the target you are looking at the Cosmic Awakening effect which can grant them access to the abilities of your Cosmic Herald.

    Cosmic Resonance

    Drain your Reality Energy to grant the Regeneration, Resistance and Strength effects on all Heralds within a 20 block radius. This will also grant the Wither Effect to all non-heralds within a 10 block radius.

    Heralds 20 blocksNon-heralds 10 blocks

    Cosmic Energy Surge

    Drain your Reality Energy to slow all targets within a 5 block radius around you, using your Cosmic Energy to deal damage to them over time.

    Radius 5 blocks

    Relevant Information

    • This power is loosely based on Franklin Richards, a.k.a. Powerhouse.
    • Cerebro gives buffs to telepathic abilities when worn.
    • This power is one of the two powers that can travel other dimensions.

    There are 4 abilities that add boosts to you when you are in the Pocket Dimension:

    Pocket Dimension: Healing+2 HP / 2 Secs
    Pocket Dimension: Health+6 HP
    Pocket Dimension: Energy Gain+30 Energy / Sec
    Pocket Dimension: Saturation+1 Saturation / 3 Secs
