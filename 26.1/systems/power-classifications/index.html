    Power Classifications

    Every power on this wiki is tagged with one or more power classifications. They show up as the colored pills in the Power classification row of each power's overview card. This page explains what each one means.

    How to read them

    A power can have several classifications. A classification describes what the power is mainly good at, not everything it can do.

    All classifications

    Offense Defense Fighter Heavy Hitter Tank Mobility Crowd Control Area Control Manipulation Negation Utility Sensory Stealth Psychic Versatile Support Survivability Transformation

    Combat

    How a power fights: dealing damage, taking it, or both.

    Offense

    Deals damage through direct attacks, ranged attacks, or large-scale destruction.

    Defense

    Prevents or reduces incoming damage through protection, resistance, barriers, or damage negation.

    Fighter

    Excels in direct combat, combining offensive capabilities with combat-oriented abilities.

    Heavy Hitter

    Specializes in delivering exceptionally high damage through powerful attacks or devastating bursts of damage.

    Tank

    Excels at absorbing sustained damage and surviving combat through high durability or defensive abilities.

    Movement & Control

    Getting around, and deciding where enemies can go and what they can do.

    Mobility

    Enhances movement through flight, teleportation, dashing, wall-crawling, or dimensional travel.

    Crowd Control

    Restricts or disrupts enemy actions through immobilization, slowing, stunning, or other disabling effects.

    Area Control

    Controls specific zones of the battlefield using persistent effects, hazards, barriers, or area denial.

    Manipulation

    Controls, alters, or redirects existing matter, energy, forces, or phenomena, including environmental and physical elements.

    Negation

    Suppresses, disables, or removes powers, abilities, or specific effects.

    Utility & Awareness

    Tools, senses, secrecy and the mind.

    Utility

    Provides practical functions beyond direct combat, such as item creation, storage, or environmental interaction.

    Sensory

    Enhances perception, detects threats, or reveals otherwise inaccessible information.

    Stealth

    Avoids detection through invisibility, concealment, disguise, or suppression of presence.

    Psychic

    Interacts with minds or mental processes through telepathy, mind reading, mental projection, or other psychic abilities.

    Versatile

    Offers a broad range of different capabilities that serve multiple purposes.

    Support & Survival

    Helping allies, staying alive, and changing form.

    Support

    Assists allies through healing, buffs, shared abilities, or protective effects.

    Survivability

    Improves the user's ability to survive threats through regeneration, absorption of damage or energy, intangibility, or other survival-enhancing abilities.

    Transformation

    Changes the user's form, appearance, physical properties, or state to gain different capabilities.

    Using them on a power page

    In a power overview card, use one <i> per classification. The class name is c- plus the name in lowercase, with a hyphen for spaces:

    <div><span>Power classification</span><b class="pc-types"><i class="c-fighter">Fighter</i><i class="c-mobility">Mobility</i><i class="c-crowd-control">Crowd Control</i></b></div>

    The icon and color come from the class, so the pill looks the same on every page.
