    Karma

    Karma can be gained or lost via killing mobs in your world. It can be raised or lowered nearly infinitely. The specific mob you kill is tied to a specific value as listed below:

    Passive Mobs

    Killing the following passive mobs will grant -1 Karma when killed:

    minecraft:cow minecraft:sheep minecraft:pig minecraft:chicken
    minecraft:rabbit minecraft:horse minecraft:donkey minecraft:mule
    minecraft:camel minecraft:cat minecraft:fox minecraft:parrot
    minecraft:frog minecraft:goat minecraft:axolotl minecraft:sniffer
    minecraft:squid minecraft:glow_squid minecraft:cod minecraft:salmon
    minecraft:bee minecraft:dolphin minecraft:wolf minecraft:mooshroom
    minecraft:llama minecraft:turtle minecraft:bat minecraft:tropical_fish

    Positive Mobs

    Killing the following positive mobs will remove the corresponding Karma when killed:

    minecraft:villager -50
    minecraft:iron_golem -50
    minecraft:wandering_trader -50
    minecraft:player -100

    Hostile Mobs

    Killing the following hostile mobs will remove the corresponding Karma when killed:

    minecraft:zombie +1 minecraft:creeper +2 minecraft:vex +3
    minecraft:husk +1 minecraft:enderman +2 minecraft:ravager +8
    minecraft:drowned +1 minecraft:phantom +2 minecraft:vindicator +3
    minecraft:skeleton +1 minecraft:pillager +2 minecraft:evoker +6
    minecraft:stray +1 minecraft:witch +3 minecraft:magma_cube +2
    minecraft:spider +1 minecraft:guardian +3 minecraft:piglin +1
    minecraft:cave_spider +1 minecraft:blaze +3 minecraft:hoglin +3
    minecraft:slime +1 minecraft:wither_skeleton +4 minecraft:zoglin +3
    minecraft:endermite +1 minecraft:ghast +4 minecraft:zombified_piglin +1
    minecraft:silverfish +1 minecraft:shulker +4 minecraft:piglin_brute +6
    minecraft:elder_guardian +8 minecraft:warden +10 minecraft:wither +20
    minecraft:ender_dragon +100

    Currently (as of v12.4.1), Karma only corresponds with being able to weild the following tertiary powers, Bonus Stats and damage on specific powers:

    • Mjolnir
    • Stormbreaker
    • Hellspawn
    • Spirit of Vengance

    If you have cheats on you can use /Fsang.Karma command to change your Karma.
