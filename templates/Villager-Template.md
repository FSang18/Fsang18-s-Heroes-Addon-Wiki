The **Blank Villager** profession can be given to an unemployed villager by placing a **Blank Block** near them. Once the job has been acquired, the following trades have a _chance_ to appear based on the Trade level of the Villager:

**Tier 1 Trades:**
- Option 1
- Option 2
- Option 3

- Option 3
