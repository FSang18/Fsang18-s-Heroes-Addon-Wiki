    Flight Types

    Flight types are used as the flight_type of a flight ability and decide how flying feels.

    2 listed · Argon 1.1.0

    Controlled Flight

    IDargon:controlled_flight
    Flight with momentum, banking and a body that leans into its travel direction. Speed still comes from "speed" and "sprint_speed". Toggling, speed levels, landing and takeoff work like palladium:flight.

    Forced Flight

    IDargon:forced_flight
    Flight matching Palladium's default movement and animation, except the entity can never land or toggle out. It only stops when the granting ability is disabled.

    KeyTypeDescriptionRequiredFallback
    speedFloatThe normal flight speed.✓/
    sprint_speedFloatThe sprinting flight speed, defaulting to the normal speed when unset.✗/
    screen_shakeScreen ShakeCamera shake while sprinting in flight. Only used when a sprint_speed is set.✗/
    propelled_soundIdentifierLooping sound played while fast-flying (sprinting), its volume scaling with flight speed.✗/
