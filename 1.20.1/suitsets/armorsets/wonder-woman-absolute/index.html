    Wonder Woman (Absolute)

    "Wonder Woman (Absolute) Suit" is obtained via transforming Wonder Woman Armorset inside Multiversal Iterator with "Elseworlds" Multiversal Extrapolator and can be repaired via "Reinforced Fabric Repair Kits".

    • Armor Rating: 20
